# Review: PR #166, the waiting-time clock

**PR:** [#166](https://github.com/Aliator/MedApp/pull/166), branch `feature/waiting-clock`, reviewed at `52556d4` on 18 November 2026. A safety review of the clock, its migration, the pause and resume endpoints and the pathway target date rule. CI (full tier) passed at that commit: https://github.com/Aliator/MedApp/actions/runs/44346108741. No finding is high or critical.

Adds each referral's waiting-time clock (`WaitingClock`: whole UK days, targets per specialty and priority, breached once past `TargetOn`), patient-initiated pauses with a review date (`POST api/referrals/{id}/clock/pause` and `…/resume`, `WorkPathways`), the template's clock-stop stage, and the rule that a referral's pathway takes its target date from the clock (`PathwayChange.FollowClock`, `"source":"clock"` audit entries; `PATCH` refuses a different date while the clock runs or is paused). Accepting no longer takes a target date. Migration `AddedWaitingTimeClock`.

**Findings:**
- **Medium, fixed:** the migration's backfill set every referral's `TargetDays` from `RequestedPriority` (`20261118161655_AddedWaitingTimeClock.cs`, the first `UPDATE`), but an accepted referral's clock follows the priority the triager chose, as accepting does (`AcceptReferralHandler.cs:75`). A referral requested Low and triaged Urgent before the deploy would have got 126 days, missed its 14-day breach, and been moved out to the Low target by `FollowClock` at its first pause or resume, with `PATCH` refusing a hand correction. Fixed: an `UPDATE` for `Status = 1` takes the days from the pathway's `Priority` by the specialty's targets before the `DATEADD` step (`AddedWaitingTimeClock.cs:130-140`); `WaitingClockMigrationTests` has a referral requested Low and accepted Urgent, which gets the Urgent target.
- **Low, documented:** during a rollout, an API copy from before this release registers referrals with `TargetDays = 0` and `TargetOn = 0001-01-01` (the columns' migration defaults), which show as breached at once with a huge wait. A safe default can't be derived from `ReceivedOn` in a column default, and treating 0 as "not set" would touch the clock, both lists' SQL and the mappers. Left as the to-do in `docs/todo.md` (Phase 2, the clock's entry): deploy before anyone registers a referral, or correct those rows' `TargetDays`, `WaitStartOn` and `TargetOn` afterwards.

**Remaining risk:** breaches and passed review dates are shown, never sent (Phase 4's scheduled job; hazards H-42, H-43). Open accepted pathways keep their pre-deploy target date until their clock next moves it. UI parity screenshots and the axe audit weren't run for the new screens.
