# Letters

Letter templates and the PDF engine (PR 15 of `docs/centre-phases-3-5-plan.md`, decisions 98 and 99), and letters themselves: writing, approving and sending (PR 16, decisions 100 to 108). Read before changing any of them. **Nothing clinical is generated:** merge fields copy identity and administrative data only; the clinical content is what the author writes (the device position, `docs/clinical-safety/intended-purpose-and-device-decision.md`).

- **A template** (`LetterTemplate`, `MedApp.Domain/Letters`; table `LetterTemplates`, migration `AddedLetterTemplates`): a name (200), a kind (`LetterKind`, stored by number: `ClinicLetter = 0`, `DischargeSummary = 1`, `ReferralResponse = 2`, `InformationRequest = 3`, `AppointmentLetter = 4`, `Other = 5`; never renumber), a specialty (`SpecialtyId`, a restricting foreign key) or null for every specialty, the letterhead (plain lines, 1,000), the body skeleton (Markdown, 20,000, `nvarchar(max)`), the closing (plain lines, 1,000), `IsArchived`, `Version` (a concurrency token) and the times. No patient data: it's an organisation's wording, so reading it records nothing (`EndpointCoverageTests`).
  - **Names are unique within a specialty, ignoring case,** and among those for every specialty: the unique index `IX_LetterTemplates_SpecialtyId_Name` has **no filter** (`HasFilter(null)`; EF would add `IS NOT NULL` for a nullable column), as SQL Server treats nulls as equal. The repository turns its violation into a 409.
  - **Deleted only while no letter was written from it** (`DELETE api/letter-templates/{id}`, `ManageSetup`, 204; 409 otherwise, and the foreign key `Letters.TemplateId` refuses a race); a used one is archived. `CanBeDeleted` on the response says which. A specialty a template is for counts it (`SpecialtyUsage.LetterTemplateCount`), so it can't be deleted, only archived.
  - Text is kept tidied (`LetterTemplateRules.Tidy`: plain line breaks, no blank end). Plain parts keep blank lines (they space the closing for a signature).
- **Merge fields** (`LetterMergeFields`, `MedApp.Application/Letters/Common`): `{{name}}`, case-sensitive, spaces allowed inside the braces. The fixed list: `patient.name`, `patient.dateOfBirth`, `patient.nhsNumber`, `patient.address`, `practice.name`, `practice.address`, `referral.receivedDate`, `appointment.lastAttendedDate`, `author.name`, `author.jobTitle`, `approver.name`, `approver.jobTitle`, `today`, `site.name`. **Never add one that reads a clinical value.**
  - An unknown name in a template is a 400 on the field holding it (`UnknownMessage`), so a typo can't reach a letter.
  - `FillPlain` (letterhead, closing) and `FillMarkdown` (the body: values escaped, so a name with `*` or `[` can't change formatting; a value's lines become hard breaks). A field with no value is left empty and listed in `Missing`: approval is refused naming it (`LetterRendering.MissingMessage`).
  - `SampleValues` are synthetic (Sam Example, 999 000 0018, ZZ postcodes), with today's real date.
- **The renderer** (`ILetterRenderer`; `PdfSharpLetterRenderer` in `MedApp.Infrastructure/Letters`, a singleton): A4 PDFs from a `LetterDocument` (title, letterhead, recipient address, Markdown body, closing, the patient's identifiers line, large print, an optional marking).
  - **Libraries:** PDFsharp and MigraDoc **6.2.x (MIT)**. **6.5.0 and later are AGPL-3.0:** Dependabot ignores them, and moving needs a licence decision. Markdig (BSD-2-Clause) parses the body, HTML off (`MarkdownToMigraDoc`: headings, paragraphs, bold, italic, nested lists with hanging markers, quotes, rules; code, links (text then address), images (their description) and HTML shown as text). Fully managed: the Dockerfile needs nothing.
  - **The font** is Atkinson Hyperlegible (SIL OFL 1.1), four TTFs embedded in the Infrastructure assembly (`Letters/Fonts`, with `NOTICE.txt` and `OFL.txt`); `LetterFonts` resolves every family to it, as the image has no system fonts. A character it lacks is drawn as `?`; **`FindUnsupportedCharacters` refuses them first** (the template and letter validators, recipients' typed names and addresses, and approval for the filled letter, the patient's name included).
  - **Every page** has the patient's identifiers and "Page n of m" in its footer (hazard H-50) and the marking, if any, in its header and footer; large print is 16 point throughout.
  - **Tagged, PDF/A not claimed:** PDFsharp's PDF/A-1a mode (tags, language en-GB, XMP, sRGB output intent). veraPDF passes it while every character is Windows-1252; others (Ł, ş, ğ) fail clause 6.3.5 (PDFsharp writes no CIDSet for the extra font). Tags are one paragraph per page plus the marking; the footer is an artifact. To-do in `docs/todo.md`.
  - **Limited:** at most two renders at a time in each API copy (`PdfSharpLetterRenderer.MaxConcurrent`). Nesting is capped (`MarkdownToMigraDoc.MaxDepth`, 8 levels of quotes and lists, then flat paragraphs at that indent, walked without recursion; `MaxInlineDepth`, 16 of emphasis and links, then plain text), and a body Markdig refuses as too deeply nested is shown as its plain lines, so no body can overflow the stack. Create, update and preview take at most 256 KB (`[RequestSizeLimit]`), and the merge field and font scans run only on text within its length.
- **Endpoints** (`LetterTemplatesController`, `api/letter-templates`): `GET` (`includeArchived`; every specialty's first, then by specialty name, then name), `GET {id}`, `GET merge-fields`: all `UseApp`. `POST` (201), `PUT {id}` (`expectedVersion`), `POST {id}/archive` and `/restore` (`{expectedVersion}`), `POST preview` (the text, saved or not, and `largePrint`; a PDF of made-up details marked "Preview with made-up details: not a letter" on every page, sent as a download through `AttachmentResponseHeaders`): all `ManageSetup`.
- **The web app:** Letter Templates (`/admin/letter-templates`, `ManageSetup`, in Administration), and its editor (`/admin/letter-templates/new`, `/$id`) with the merge fields listed and Download Preview (PDF).
- **`medapp-dev`:** `reset_demo_data` deletes letters and templates (`DemoWipe`); the Phase 3 demo data (PR 17) seeds them.

## Letters (PR 16)

**A letter is clinical correspondence written by a person.** MedApp fills identity and administrative merge fields only; the body is the author's own, and nothing in it is generated (the device position).
- **A letter** (`Letter`, `LetterRecipient`, `MedApp.Domain/Letters`; tables `Letters` and `LetterRecipients`, migration `AddedLetters`): the patient, **one** pathway or referral it's about (restricting foreign keys), the template it came from (ID, a restricting key, and its name then), the kind, its own copy of the letterhead, body (Markdown, 20,000, `nvarchar(max)`) and closing, large print, the status, author, approver, the return comment (500) and who returned it when, the approved PDF (`PdfAttachmentId`), the withdrawal (who, when, reason, 500), `ReplacesLetterId` (a correction), `Version` and a row version. Recipients (`Position` 0 is the one it's addressed to; the rest are copies listed after the closing): kind, `PracticeId` (no foreign key: the name and address are copied), name, address, postcode, secure email, dispatch status, method, `SentOn`, who and when, and the relay's message ID.
- **Stored enums, never renumbered:** `LetterStatus` (`Draft = 0`, `AwaitingApproval = 1`, `Approved = 2`, `Withdrawn = 3`; a returned letter is a Draft with a return comment), `LetterRecipientKind` (`GpPractice = 0`, `Patient = 1`, `Referrer = 2`, `Other = 3`), `LetterDispatchStatus` (`NotSent = 0`, `Sent = 1`, `Failed = 2`), `LetterDispatchMethod` (`Post = 0`, `HandedToPatient = 1`, `SecureEmail = 2`, `EmailOutsideMedApp = 3`, `Other = 4`; `GpConnect = 5` is Phase 5's). Audit actions `LetterDrafted = 29` to `LetterWithdrawn = 34`; `ActivityAction.LetterViewed = 47`; `NotificationKind` `LetterAwaitingApproval = 29`, `LetterApproved = 30`, `LetterReturned = 31`.
- **Where the rules live** (`MedApp.Application/Letters/Common`): `LetterWorkflow` (who may do what in each status, every message), `LetterChange` (**the only way to change a letter's status or sending**: bumps `Version` and writes its audit entries, saved in the same `SaveChangesAsync`), `LetterRecords` (loads a letter with its patient and pathway or referral; the merge values; recipients filled from the record; the page's response), `LetterRendering` (the filled `LetterDocument`), `LetterMapper`, `LetterNotifications`, `LetterValidation`, `ILetterTransport` (`SecureLetterEmails`: the email's words). `ILetterRepository` shares the request's DbContext, so approval saves the letter, its PDF's attachment record and both audit entries together.
- **Permissions** (`Letters` group): `WriteLetters` (start, edit, submit, discard, correct), `ApproveLetters` (approve or return; approve your own draft), `SendLetters` (secure email, record sent). Starting roles: Clinician and Senior clinician all three, Nurse write and send, Booking send; **Admin none** (clinical: `Permissions.NotForAdmin`). Reading, the preview and withdrawing need `ViewPatients`, the handler checking who.
- **Writing** (`POST api/letters` `{templateId, pathwayId | referralId}`, 201): the template must be the pathway's or referral's specialty's or every specialty's (400), not archived (409); from a referral only a referral response or a request for more information (400), and not on one entered in error (409). It copies the template's text; recipients start as the patient's registered practice and the patient (from a referral: the referrer, and the patient for a reply). Nothing is suggested beyond that.
  - **A draft is its author's alone** (`PUT api/letters/{id}` `{letterhead, body, closing, largePrint, recipients, expectedVersion}`; 403 for anyone else, 409 once submitted): text checked like a template's (lengths, known merge fields, the font); at most 10 recipients (one patient); GP practice from the directory (`practiceId`), Other with a name and address (and optionally a secure email in an allowed domain), a referrer's address typed only when its referral names no directory practice. Drafts aren't audited edit by edit: the approved PDF is the record.
  - **Preview** (`GET api/letters/{id}/preview`): the draft filled from the record now, marked "Draft, not approved" on every page, a download; for its author, approver or any holder of `ApproveLetters`; records `LetterViewed` with `preview`.
- **Approval** (decision 102): `POST …/submit` `{approverUserId, expectedVersion}` to someone else who holds `ApproveLetters` (400 otherwise; told: `LetterAwaitingApproval`); `POST …/return` `{comment, expectedVersion}` by that approver (the author told, without the comment); `POST …/approve` `{expectedVersion}` by that approver, or the author for their own draft if they hold the permission. Approval checks, in order: the approver's full name (409), recipients, each with an address but the patient (409; names and addresses copied again now), every merge field with a value (409, naming them), every character printable (409). Then it renders the PDF (`ILetterRenderer`), stores it as an `Attachment` on the pathway (or the referral), with an `AttachmentAdded` and a `LetterApproved` audit entry, in one save (the blob deleted again if the save fails); the author is told unless they approved it.
- **Sending, per recipient** (decision 103):
  - **Secure email** (`POST …/recipients/{rid}/send` `{expectedVersion}`): only while `ILetterTransport.IsAvailable` (`Letters:SecureEmail` with a host and from address **and** `RelayMeetsDcb1596=true`, and outside Development a `Security` other than `None`), only to an address in `Letters:SecureEmailDomains` (`nhs.net` by default), **never to the patient**. The letter is claimed first (a version bump saved, so two senders can't both email it), then sent **synchronously** with MailKit (`SecureEmailLetterTransport`, not the `IEmailSender` queue): marked Sent only when the relay accepts it; a refusal is recorded as Failed (an audit entry too) and a 409 says so. The email says only "A letter from {centre}. It's attached." with `letter.pdf`; logs carry the failure's category, never the address. In development the AppHost points it at Mailpit; outside Development it's off until a deployment configures it (not in the chart yet: `docs/todo.md`).
  - **Recorded** (`POST …/record-sent` `{method, sentOn, expectedVersion}`): Post, HandedToPatient (the patient only), EmailOutsideMedApp (not to the patient) or Other, on a UK date from the approval day, up to 7 days back, not in the future.
- **Withdrawing and correcting** (decision 104): `POST …/withdraw` `{reason, expectedVersion}` by whoever approved it or a holder of `CorrectRecords`, allowed while the record is restricted (it protects the patient), not once anonymised; the PDF stays (and can't be deleted on its own: `DeleteAttachmentHandler`, 409). `POST …/correction` (201) starts a new draft with its text and recipients, once. `POST …/discard` withdraws the author's own draft (no reason), so an abandoned draft doesn't block anonymisation.
- **The gate** (decision 105; the results gates, decision 95, are beside it in `StageGates`: [Results](results.md)): a stage definition's `NeedsLetterKind` (copied onto each pathway's stage, like `VisitRequirement`; on the definition, not the template's stage map, decided so) stops the stage being finished until a letter of that kind on the pathway is approved and sent to every recipient (`StageGates.WhyLetterMissing`, `FinishPathwayStageHandler`; 409). No override.
- **Lists:** `GET api/letters/search` (`ViewPatients`; `LetterSearch`, LINQ that runs in SQL and in memory, ordered by dates then ID): views `MyDrafts`, `AwaitingMyApproval`, `ReturnedToMe`, `ToSend`, `Sent`, or every letter of a `patientId`, `pathwayId` or `referralId`; records `PatientListed`. `GET api/letters/count` (the side nav: mine to approve, all to send) records nothing.
- **Privacy** ([privacy](privacy.md)): `GET api/letters/{id}` records `LetterViewed` (`{letterId}`). The audit holds IDs, kinds, the template's name, usernames, recipients' kinds, methods and dispatch statuses, never the text, comment, reason or anyone's name or address. Staff's timeline (kind `Letters`) shows those; admins also see the return comment and withdrawal reason, read from the letter. Export: every letter with its text and recipients. Anonymisation refused while a letter is a draft, awaiting approval, or not sent to everyone; then the body, closing, comment, reason and every recipient but a directory practice lose their names and addresses. A restricted record's letters can't be started, edited, approved or sent (`LetterWorkflow.RestrictedMessage`, asking an admin). A pathway with letters can't be deleted.
- **The web app:** Letters (`/letters`, side nav with the count), a letter's page (`/letters/$id`: the editor, recipients, approval, sending, withdrawal), Letters cards on the pathway and referral pages with Write letter, Needs a letter on the stage library, Delete on unused templates.
