> **How [Company name] uses personal information**
>
> **Who we are.** [Company name] ("we") makes MedApp, a system that clinics and hospitals use to coordinate patients' procedures. We are at [Address], company number [company number], and registered with the Information Commissioner's Office under [ICO registration number]. Our data protection officer is [DPO name], [DPO contact].
>
> **If you're a patient, or work for one of our customers.** Your information in MedApp belongs to the clinic or hospital that uses it, and they are responsible for it. We process it only on their instructions, in the UK. Please contact them about it. If you contact us, we'll pass your message to them within 2 working days and tell you who they are.
>
> **What we hold, why, and our legal basis:**
>
> | Who | What | Why | Legal basis |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Contacts at our customers and prospective customers | Name, job title, work email and phone, organisation | To provide our service, manage our contracts, and tell you about MedApp | Our contract with your organisation; our legitimate interests |
> | People who contact us for support | Name, work email, your message | To answer you | Our contract; our legitimate interests |
> | People who complain or make a data protection request | Your details and correspondence | To handle it, as the law requires | Legal obligation |
> | Job applicants | Your application and interview notes | To recruit | Steps before a contract; legitimate interests |
> | Visitors to our website | [Server logs only] | [To run and secure the website] | Legitimate interests |
>
> **Marketing.** We send business contacts information about MedApp only where the law allows, and every message lets you opt out.
>
> **Who we share it with.** Our service providers (email, accounts, [helpdesk]), under contracts; our professional advisers; and authorities where the law requires. We don't sell personal information.
>
> **Where it's kept.** In the UK, or where our service providers are, with the safeguards UK law requires. [List any providers outside the UK and the safeguard.]
>
> **How long.** Contracts and accounts: 6 years. Support requests: 2 years. Complaints: 3 years. Marketing contacts: until you opt out, or 2 years without contact. Applicants: 6 months.
>
> **Your rights.** You can ask for a copy of your information, and ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict it, or move it, and object to how we use it, including marketing. Contact [Privacy contact email]. We'll reply within one month.
>
> **Complaints.** Contact our DPO at [DPO contact], or use [complaints form URL]. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 5 working days. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/). Our complaints procedure: [complaints.md](complaints.md).
