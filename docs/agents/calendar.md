# Calendar and appointments

Staff calendars, templates, meetings, appointments and stage visits: read before changing any of them.

The concept and decisions are in `docs/calendar-plan.md`. Each member of staff's **calendar** holds **appointments** with patients and **blocks** of time for activities; **day and week templates** fill each week with its usual blocks; a stage can **involve a visit**, and the app then asks for an appointment before it's finished.
- **The rules live in two `Common` folders.** Use them; don't set an appointment's `Status`, or work out UK times, slots or clashes, in a handler by hand.
  - `MedApp.Application/Calendar/Common`: `WorkingHours` (the fixed rules: the default day, the 06:00 to 23:00 bounds, 5-minute boundaries, slot length, messages; `WhyOutside(startUtc, endUtc, closedDays)` for what's nobody's), `WorkingTime` and `WorkingTimeSet` (one person's working day on a date, from their hours, their site's and the closed days), `WorkingDays` and `WorkingDayRules` (those with the closed days and hours looked up, for validators: `InsideWorkingDay` with the person, `WorkingDay`), `GridWindow` (the clock times one response's grid shows), `StaffHoursRules`, `HoursValidation`, `WorkingHoursMapper`, `BankHolidays` (England and Wales, worked out in code), `MeetingReplies`, `UkTime` (UK dates and clock times to and from UTC, and dates for messages), `TemplateHistory` (replacing dated rows from today), `CalendarProjection` (a person's days, with template blocks and exceptions, from the repository's `CalendarData`), `CalendarClashes` (what a time would overlap), `CalendarMapper` and `CalendarSetupMapper`.
  - `MedApp.Application/Appointments/Common`: `AppointmentWorkflow` (statuses and what's allowed from each), `AppointmentChange` (applies a change and writes its history) and `AppointmentMapper`.
- **Working hours** (centre plan decisions 119 to 122, PR 22): each person's working day is their own hours, or the default, **Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 UK time, at no particular site**, for anyone with none (so nothing changed on deploy). It's built per person and date by `WorkingTimeSet.For` (`WorkingTime`), and every check uses it: validators (`WorkingDays`), `CalendarClashes.Find` under the lock, `CalendarProjection`, `Availability` and the walk-in rule.
  - **Staff hours** (`StaffHours`: a day of the week, starts, ends and the site; dated rows kept with `TemplateHistory`, so past days keep what applied): a person with rows valid on a date works only the days they name. **Site hours** (`SiteHours`: a day, opens and closes, dated the same way): a site with none is open the default; one with rows only on the days they name. A person's day is the part of their hours their site is open that day (none if it's closed that weekday). An evening clinic or a Saturday morning is just hours.
  - **Bounds:** any day of the week, between 06:00 and 23:00 UK time (`WorkingHours.Earliest`, `Latest`), on 5-minute boundaries, the end after the start, so a working day still never spans a clock change. Day template blocks share the bounds.
  - **Who:** `GET`/`PUT api/calendar/staff/{userId}/hours` (anyone reads anyone's, as calendars show them; set by holders of `ManageClinics` or `ManageAnyCalendar`, `CalendarActor.CanSetHours`, 403 otherwise, never by the person alone). `GET`/`PUT api/sites/{id}/hours` (`UseApp` reads, `ManageSetup` sets). A `PUT` replaces the set from today; no days goes back to the default. Hours outside their site's that day, or at a site closed that weekday, are a 409 (`StaffHoursRules`); an archived site can't be chosen unless they already work there. Site hours that would leave anyone's current hours outside them are a 409 naming who; so is archiving a site anyone works at, and such a site can't be deleted. Changing someone's hours takes their and the sites' calendar locks; changing a site's, the site's.
  - **What's already on a calendar stays** when hours change, as with templates; the grid widens to show it.
  - **The working day's rule:** an appointment, block or meeting must start and end on the same day, on a 5-minute boundary, inside the person's working day (one of their days, inside their hours, not closed): a 400. The validator checks the person when the command names them (`InsideWorkingDay(end, staff, workingDays)`); moving a block checks its owner in the handler (400), and a meeting each attendee in `MeetingStaff.EnsureEveryoneFreeAsync` (a 400 naming them; a closed day stays a 409). Handlers check again under the lock (`CalendarClashes.Find`, 409).
  - **The calendar response:** each day carries `isWorkingDay`, `hasHours` (one of their days, closed or not), `siteId`, `workStartUtc` and `workEndUtc` (their hours; the default's on a day not worked) and `gridStartUtc`/`gridEndUtc` (`GridWindow`: the same clock times on every day of one calendar or team, from the earliest working start, or anything on the calendars, to the latest end, in whole hours). The grid shows days they work at full width and shades times outside their hours; a team's "Everyone free" day is worked when everyone is (`CalendarProjection.TeamDay`).
  - **Week templates stay Monday to Friday:** a weekend day someone works gets template blocks only through Apply Template (a day override) (`docs/todo.md`).
  - Shown in 15-minute rows or 5-minute ones (the Calendar page's Rows menu, `rows=5` in its URL, each person's last choice kept).
- **Sites and rooms** (`Site`, `Room`, `MedApp.Application/Sites`, `api/sites` and `api/rooms`; centre plan decision 118): where the centre works, admin-managed (Administration, Sites and Rooms), read by staff. Every database has **Main site** (`Site.MainSiteId`, `HasData`), which admins rename; there's always at least one open site (`SiteRules.EnsureNotLastOpenSite`, 409). Names are unique (rooms within their site), by unique indexes too. A used one is archived, not deleted: a site with rooms or closed days; a room a clinic session, pattern or appointment names (`ISiteRepository.GetRoomsInUseAsync`). A site with open rooms can't be archived, and an archived site takes no new rooms or closed days, nor restored rooms (409). Address (up to three lines), postcode (`UkPostcodes`) and phone are optional, and the organisation's, never a person's.
- **Clinic sessions** (`ClinicSession`, `ClinicPattern`, `MedApp.Application/Clinics`, `api/clinics`; centre plan decisions 123 to 127, PR 23): a clinician's time in a room for a specialty, cut into slots of 5 to 120 minutes (multiples of 5, dividing it exactly), where appointments go, one patient per slot. Anyone signed in reads them (`UseApp`); holders of `ManageClinics` add, change and cancel sessions and keep the patterns. The rules are in `ClinicRules` (slots, the room's day), `SessionPlacement` (a session's checks) and `AppointmentPlaces` (booking into a slot or a room).
  - **A session is busy time** on its clinician's calendar (`CalendarData.Sessions`, in `CalendarClashes.BusyTimes`, so blocks, meetings, `Availability` and appointments outside it clash with it: "That time overlaps a clinic session in Room 2"). It's inside their working day and its room's site's opening hours, in the future, and not on a day closed everywhere or at the room's site. The calendar response carries each day's sessions (`sessions`), drawn on the grid beside their appointments.
  - **A room holds one session at a time:** its lock (`ICalendarLock` with the room's ID, beside the clinician's: the lock takes any IDs, sorted, as a site's hours already do) and its clash check (`ClinicRules.WhyRoomUnavailable`: the site closed that day or shut then, another session, or an appointment that names the room). Integration tests race two sessions for one room.
  - **Booking into a slot:** `POST api/appointments` and `.../book` take `clinicSessionId` (and optionally `roomId` alone, for an appointment outside a session, clash-checked under the room's lock too). The member of staff must be the session's clinician, the time exactly one of its slots, the session Planned, and its site not closed that day (409 otherwise); the appointment takes the session's room. The clinician's lock serialises a slot, `CalendarClashes.Find(..., ignoreSessionId)` sees any appointment already in it, and the filtered unique index `IX_Appointments_ClinicSessionId_StartUtc_OnCalendar` (session and start, Booked, Attended or Did not attend) is the last word (`ClinicRules.SlotTaken`, a 409). Integration tests race two patients for one slot. The history records the session's and room's IDs (`clinicSessionId`, `roomId`, and `previous…` on a move), never names; moving an appointment anywhere else takes it out of the session.
  - **Changing a session** (`PUT api/clinics/sessions/{id}`, with `expectedLastUpdated`; a row version too): its room, specialty, time and slot length; never its clinician (cancel and recreate). With appointments in it, only the room (if free; its Booked appointments move with it, each recorded as a move in its history, while those attended or missed keep the room they happened in) and the specialty (409 `ClinicRules.HasBookings` otherwise). Not on a past day.
  - **Cancelling** (`POST .../cancel` `{reason, expectedLastUpdated}`): Booked appointments in it that haven't started are cancelled with the reason through `AppointmentChange`, in the same save (one that has started, and Attended and Did not attend ones, stay for their outcome), and the response lists the patients to tell. The list's `PatientListed` is recorded inside the cancellation's transaction, so if the log fails the cancellation is rolled back. The cancelled session's detail (`GET .../{id}`) lists them again (`patientsToTell`: the appointments cancelled with it, stamped with its `CancelledAtUtc`). The clinician is told once (`ClinicSessionCancelled = 39`, a fixed title, the room and time; never the reason or a patient), unless they cancelled it; nobody gets `AppointmentCancelled` for each. With `tellPatients`, each is texted or emailed (below). Stage visits follow the rule for any cancelled appointment.
  - **Patterns make sessions only when asked** (`POST api/clinics/patterns/{id}/create-sessions` `{weeks}`, 1 to 13): each date (its weekday, every 1 to 4 weeks from the first such day on or after its from date, until its until date) is checked as a new session is, under the same locks, and skipped with a reason (already made, already started, closed, not a working day, a clash, the room taken or archived). A pattern makes at most one session on a date (`IX_ClinicSessions_PatternId_Date`, cancelled ones included). Changing a pattern leaves the sessions it made.
  - **Reads:** `GET api/clinics/sessions?from&to` (at most 62 days; `withFreeSlots` for booking, with each session's `freeSlotStartsUtc`) names no patient and records nothing; `GET api/clinics/sessions/{id}` lists who is in each slot and records `PatientListed`.
  - **Pages:** Clinics (`/calendar/clinics`, a week's sessions by day, time and room, from the Calendar page), Clinic Patterns (`/calendar/clinics/patterns`, `ManageClinics`), each session's dialog (slots, Book on a free one, Edit, Cancel Session with the list to tell), and the booking dialog's **Clinic slots** (sessions of the pathway's specialty with free slots, for four weeks).
- **Closed days** (`ClosedDay`: a UK date, a name and optionally a site (`SiteId`, none for everywhere); one everywhere per date and one per site per date (`IX_ClosedDays_Date_SiteId`, unfiltered so the nulls count as equal); `docs/calendar-plan.md`, decisions 72 to 75; centre plan decision 121): bank holidays and any other day the clinic is closed.
  - **A site's closed day** closes that site's rooms and the working day of staff whose hours put them there that date (`WorkingTimeSet.For` uses `ClosedDaySet.NameAt`); someone on the default works at no particular site, so only days closed everywhere close theirs. `ClosedDaySet.IsClosed`/`NameOn` are about days closed everywhere, and `IsClosedAt`/`NameAt` add a site's own. Adding a site's closed day still reports no bookings and tells nobody (`docs/todo.md`). A site can't be closed on a date closed everywhere (409); everywhere can be added over sites' days, and bank holidays ignore them. Adding one reports no bookings and tells nobody. The web app treats only days without a `siteId` as closed for everyone. Admins keep the list (Administration, Closed Days; `POST`/`DELETE api/calendar/closed-days`, and `POST .../bank-holidays { year }` adding England and Wales's from `BankHolidays`, skipping dates already closed and past ones); anyone reads it (`GET api/calendar/closed-days?from&to`). Adding or removing one before today is a 409.
  - **Like a day not worked, for everything new:** the working day rule's 400 for every command (validators use `WorkingDays`, never `WorkingHours.IsWorkingDay` alone), no template blocks (`CalendarProjection`), no free or suggested times (`Availability`), and the day is `isWorkingDay: false` with `closedDayName` ("Closed: Christmas Day"). `CalendarData.ClosedDays` carries the range's closed days.
  - **What's already on it stays,** flagged as on a closed day; the app never moves or cancels it. Closing a day says what's on it (`GET api/calendar/closed-days/bookings?date`, and `Bookings` on the add responses) and tells each member of staff with something on it (`DayClosed`, `ClosedDayBookings`), never the admin who closed it.
  - **`WorkingHours.WhyOutside` takes the closed days explicitly** (`ClosedDaySet.None` only where they deliberately don't count); `WorkingDaysConventionTests` checks every command validator with a `StartUtc` and `EndUtc` takes `WorkingDays`, a walk-in's too (for the person's hours, closed days left out: `closedDaysCount: false`). Handlers check the working day again under the calendar lock (`CalendarClashes.Find`).
- **Times are UTC** ([Data access](../../AGENTS.md#data-access)). Template times are UK clock times (`TimeOnly`), turned into UTC for each date by `UkTime`. Each day in the calendar response has `workStartUtc` and `workEndUtc`, so the browser steps through slots in UTC and never does time zone arithmetic. A UK working day never spans a clock change.
- **Activity types** (`CalendarActivityType`, admin-managed) are what time can be blocked for: a name and a colour from a fixed palette of Astryx `Token` colours (`red`, `orange`, `yellow`, `green`, `teal`, `cyan`, `blue`, `purple`, `pink`, `gray`). Seeded: Lunch, Admin time, Training, Meeting, Annual leave and Travel. Once used, one is archived, not deleted: new blocks can't choose it, existing ones keep it.
- **Blocks** (`CalendarBlock`): a member of staff, an activity type, a start and end, and an optional note (200 characters). Blocks are about staff, never patients: a note must not hold patient details, and the UI says so.
- **No overlaps.** A new or moved appointment, block or meeting may not overlap an appointment (Booked, Attended or Did not attend), a block (stored or from a template) or a meeting on that person's calendar (except one they declined): a 409 naming the clash (for a meeting, who has what), and the UI offers only free slots. `Availability` (shared free time, suggested meeting times) uses the same busy time (`CalendarClashes.BusyTimes`), so the server accepts every suggestion.
  - **Races for one slot** are serialised by a database lock per member of staff (`ICalendarLock`, SQL Server's `sp_getapplock`). Any command that adds or moves something on a calendar takes it before checking for clashes. It protects a calendar, not an appointment (booking one appointment with two people takes two locks), so appointments have their own protection (below).
  - **A later template change can overlap what's there:** both show, the appointment on top, the block flagged as overlapping. The app never moves or removes either.
- **Templates are applied when a calendar is read, never generated** (`CalendarProjection`), so an edit changes every day it applies to from then on. Nothing runs in the background.
  - **A day template** (`DayTemplate`) is named, with blocks (`DayTemplateBlock`: activity type, start and end time, optional note). **A week template** (`WeekTemplate`) gives each weekday a day template or none (`WeekTemplateDay`). Each member of staff may have a **default week** (`StaffWeekTemplate`).
  - **Templates are personal** (`OwnerUserId`): week templates, day overrides and the default week use only the owner's own (404 for someone else's); names are unique per owner. Each person's page is `/calendar/templates`.
  - **History is kept by dating rows, not copying them.** Day template blocks, week template days and default weeks have `ValidFrom` and `ValidTo` (UK dates). An edit closes changed rows on yesterday and adds new ones from today; unchanged rows stay; a row added and changed again today is replaced (`TemplateHistory`). So a past day shows what applied then, and a change shows from today, including today's past blocks.
  - **Changing one day's template block** stores an exception for that date (`CalendarBlockException`) hiding it, plus, for an edit, a stored block with the new values. A later template edit keeps exceptions on blocks it didn't change.
  - **Apply Template** (the Calendar page, on any working days of the week shown) stores a day override (`CalendarDayOverride`) per day: that date uses the chosen day template, or none, instead of the week's. "The default week's" removes it (the dialog treats the API's 404 for a day with no override as done). Blocks added by hand stay.
  - **Past days don't change:** blocks, exceptions and overrides before today (UK) can't be added, edited or removed (409). Past appointments can still have their outcome recorded.
  - **Templates in use are archived, not deleted,** like stage definitions: a day template once a week template or day override has referred to it, a week template once it's been anyone's default. Archived ones can't be chosen again but keep applying.
- **Who changes what** (also in [Auth](auth.md)): any staff member sees every calendar and books, reschedules, cancels and records the outcome of any appointment, for anyone. A calendar's blocks, day overrides and default week, and a person's day and week templates: the owner or an admin (`CalendarGuards.EnsureCanChangeTemplatesOf`, 403 otherwise). Activity types, meetings, the team view and suggested times: admins. Staff read them all. Each attendee accepts or declines their own meeting invitation; nobody else can, admins included.
- **A meeting** (`Meeting`, `MeetingAttendee`), arranged by an admin: a title, notes about staff (never patients), a time inside one working day, not on a past day, and at least one attendee, as busy time on each attendee's calendar.
  - Adding or changing one holds every attendee's lock (on a change, also those going before). It has a row version, and a change must carry the `lastUpdated` the caller saw (`expectedLastUpdated`): a 409 if it changed since, so a stale dialog or drag can't undo another admin's change.
  - Only attendees a change newly adds must be staff; one already going whose account has gone is dropped on save, untold. Attendees are told (`MeetingNotifications`), never of their own change. Cancelling deletes it. An account deleted, or losing every staff role, is taken off upcoming meetings (`IMeetingRepository.RemoveAttendeeFromUpcomingAsync`); a meeting left with nobody goes.
  - **Replies** (`MeetingAttendee.Reply`, a `MeetingReply`: `NoReply = 0`, `Accepted = 1`, `Declined = 2`, stored, so never renumbered; with `RepliedAtUtc`): each attendee replies for themselves (`PUT api/calendar/meetings/{id}/reply`) until the meeting ends, and can change their mind; anyone else is a 403, a cancelled meeting a 404 (`MeetingReplies`). A reply isn't a change to the meeting (`LastUpdated` stays, no `expectedLastUpdated`), but carries `expectedStartUtc` and `expectedEndUtc` (required): a 409 if the meeting has moved since. It holds the replier's calendar lock.
  - **A declined meeting isn't busy time** on the decliner's calendar (`CalendarClashes.BusyTimes`, so free and suggested times too; in the web app `busyTimes` in `calendarSlots.ts` and the grid's taken rows) and shows faded, saying Declined. Accepting again is a 409 naming what's at that time, if anything.
  - **Those who declined never stop a change:** their calendars aren't checked. **Moving a meeting** puts every reply back to `NoReply` ("Meeting moved" asks them again), except those who declined and are busy at the new time, who keep their decline and are told so (`MeetingReplies.ResetAfterMove`). Other changes keep replies. A decline tells the organiser (`MeetingDeclined`); accepting tells nobody. Rolling this back needs a step first: `docs/calendar-plan.md`, decision 71, Rolling back.
- **An appointment** (`Appointment`) is for a patient, optionally one of their pathways and its stage, with a member of staff, a start and end, a status, optional notes (2,000 characters of free text about the patient, treated like pathway notes), and who requested, booked and recorded its outcome, and when.
  - **Statuses** (`AppointmentStatus`): `Requested = 0` (shown as "Requires appointment": no staff or time yet; listed at `/calendar/requires-appointment`, reached from the Calendar page's "Requires Appointment (N)" link, not the side nav), `Booked = 1`, `Attended = 2`, `DidNotAttend = 3`, `Cancelled = 4`, `EnteredInError = 5` (below).
  - **Changes** (`AppointmentWorkflow`): Requested to Booked, and Booked to Booked (rescheduling), only in the future and clash-free. Booked to Attended or Did not attend once it has started; those two can be corrected either way. Requested or Booked to Cancelled, with a reason (500 characters). Cancelled is final: a patient who didn't attend gets a new appointment. Nothing can be booked in the past: one that already happened is recorded after the event (below).
  - **Recorded after the event** (a walk-in, or one booked outside MedApp; `POST api/appointments/past`, `RecordPastAppointmentHandler`, decisions 74 to 78): straight to Attended or Did not attend through `AppointmentChange.RecordAfterEvent`, flagged `IsRecordedAfterEvent`, the recorder as booker.
    - **When:** started, within the last 30 days (`BookingTimes.RecordAfterEventDays`), inside the person's working day that date (one of their days, their hours; a closed day is allowed) (400); for a pathway, not before the UK day it was created (`AppointmentWorkflow.IsBeforePathway`), and for its current stage or an earlier one (409).
    - **Overlaps:** not another of that person's appointments (409, `CalendarClashes.FindAppointment`, under the calendar lock); blocked time or a meeting is allowed and shows flagged (`overlapsAppointment`).
    - **For a stage,** its visit in Requires Appointment **becomes** the recorded one, keeping its notes (so one open appointment per stage holds, and an Attended one satisfies a Required stage); a stage with a visit Booked is a 409.
    - **Entered in error:** its recorder withdraws a wrong one with a reason within the same 30 days, an admin at any time (`POST api/appointments/{id}/entered-in-error`; `canMarkEnteredInError` is an admin's, `canRecorderMarkEnteredInError` its recorder's): status `EnteredInError = 5`, off the calendar, history `EnteredInError = 8`, and the stage's visit asked for again when it would be (`StageVisits.AfterVisitWithdrawn`), unless the record can't change (restricted: none is added, and the response says why). A visit asked for at the same moment is a 409 to try again. If it was a finished Required stage's only attended visit, the pathway's assignees and every admin but the withdrawer are told (`StageVisitWithdrawn = 22`), since only an admin can unlock the stage. Hazard H-39.
    - The member of staff is told when someone else recorded it (`AppointmentRecorded`).
  - **One open appointment per stage** (Requested or Booked); another is a 409 pointing to it (`AppointmentWorkflow.StageHasOpenVisit`). No limit without a stage. `CreateAppointmentHandler` checks it, and the filtered unique index `IX_Appointments_StageId_Open` stops two at once: the repository turns its refusal into `StageHasOpenAppointmentException`, the handler into the same 409. A pathway change whose automatic visit loses that race is a 409 to try again (`PatientPathwayRepository`).
  - **Two saves at once:** `Appointment.RowVersion` makes the later a 409 (`AppointmentWorkflow.ChangedMeanwhileMessage`, or `PathwayAppointmentChangedMeanwhileMessage` from a pathway change), never a silent overwrite. Every appointment save goes through `SaveChangesOrConflictAsync` with that message.
  - **Booking says what it expects:** `POST api/appointments/{id}/book` takes `expectedStatus`, the status the caller saw (`Requested` to book, `Booked` to move); any other is a 409 (`BookedMeanwhileMessage` when someone booked it first), so a stale "book" can't move someone else's booking. The web app sends its copy's status.
  - **Every change is recorded** in its history (`AppointmentEvent`, action `AppointmentEventAction`): requested, booked, rescheduled (with the old time and member of staff), attended, did not attend, cancelled (with the reason), notes changed (old and new text), recorded after the event (`RecordedAfterEvent = 7`, with the outcome) and entered in error (`EnteredInError = 8`, with the reason). No foreign keys, like the pathway audit log. New appointment commands must go through `AppointmentChange`.
  - Status, action and requirement numbers are stored, so never renumber `AppointmentStatus`, `AppointmentEventAction` or `VisitRequirement`.
- **Stage visits:**
  - **`VisitRequirement`** on stage definitions: `None = 0` (the default), `Suggested = 1` or `Required = 2`, set by admins, copied onto each stage when a pathway is created.
  - **The Requested appointment:** when a stage with a visit becomes current, `PathwayChange.RequestVisit` creates a Requested appointment for it (requested by the app), unless it already has a Requested, Booked or Attended one. None while the pathway is blocked or closed.
  - **Required:** finishing needs a linked Attended appointment, checked on the server (`PathwayWorkflow.WhyStageNeedsVisit`); staff get a 409.
  - **The admin bypass:** an admin may finish a Required stage without a visit, after confirming with a reason (the finish endpoint's optional body, `FinishPathwayStageRequest { withoutVisit: true, reason }`; with no body it works as before). The `StageFinished` audit entry records `withoutVisit`, the requirement and the reason.
  - **Suggested:** finishing without an Attended appointment asks for confirmation in the UI only; the server doesn't refuse, and `StageFinished` records `withoutVisit` and the requirement.
  - Finishing a stage again after an admin unlocked it doesn't ask for a visit again.
  - **Tidying up:** finishing a stage cancels its Requested appointments, and cancelling a pathway all of its Requested ones (`PathwayChange.CancelRequestedVisit`), each with a reason. The app never cancels a Booked one: someone may need to tell the patient, so the pathway page lists them.
  - All of this is saved with the pathway change, in one `SaveChangesAsync`.
- **Patient reminders and messages** (`MedApp.Application/PatientMessages`; centre plan decisions 128 to 133, PR 26; DPIA risk 32; hazards H-53, H-54):
  - **Every message is a stored row** (`PatientMessage`, table `PatientMessages`, no foreign keys): the patient, the appointment, the kind (`PatientMessageKind`: `AppointmentReminder = 0`, `AppointmentMoved = 1`, `AppointmentCancelled = 2`, `MissedAppointment = 3`), the channel (`PatientMessageChannel`: `Text = 0`, `Email = 1`), the destination and text as sent, the appointment's start, the status (`PatientMessageStatus`: `Pending = 0`, `Sent = 1`, `Delivered = 2`, `Failed = 3`, `Cancelled = 4`, `NotSent = 5`), a fixed reason code (`PatientMessageReasons`, or the provider's status name), the provider's reference and who asked. All stored by number: never renumber them. Unique per appointment, kind, channel, appointment start and room (`IX_PatientMessages_AppointmentId_Kind_Channel_AppointmentStartUtc_AppointmentRoomId`, unfiltered, so no room counts as one value), so a run cut short or a reschedule never sends twice, and a move to another room (perhaps another site) is a message of its own. A row version stops the job's save overwriting an anonymisation made meanwhile (the anonymisation wins). A `NotSent` reminder makes way for a new one once the patient can be reached (`IPatientMessageRepository.AddAsync`).
  - **Who gets what:** one message per channel the patient chose (`Patient.ReminderChannel`: `None = 0`, `Text = 1`, `Email = 2`, `TextAndEmail = 3`, on the patient form; `PatientMessageRules.ChannelsFor`) for which they have a mobile or email; no choice, or no contact, is stored as `NotSent` (`NoConsent`, `NoMobile`, `NoEmail`), so the appointment shows "No reminder: no consent or contact" (`PatientMessageFactory`).
  - **What it says** (`PatientMessageTexts`): the organisation (`PatientMessages:SenderName`), the date and time (UK), the site of the appointment's room (none without a room) with its address and phone, or `PatientMessages:ContactPhone`, and a fixed phrase. **Never** the specialty, clinician, pathway, stage, notes, a cancellation's reason or the NHS number. A text is 160 characters where it can be (the address lines go first). An email has a fixed subject per kind.
  - **The sender** (`IPatientMessageTransport`, chosen by `PatientMessages:Mode`): `Off` (the default: nothing is made, sent or offered), `Offline` (Development's default; `OfflinePatientMessageTransport` emails each to `PatientMessages:OfflineRecipient`, which must end `.invalid`, `.test` or `localhost` (`PatientMessageOptions.IsSafeOfflineRecipient`, checked at startup and on each send), through the staff email settings, so Mailpit catches it; never to the patient; a mobile ending 0666 or an address ending `+fail` fails, for demonstrations), `Live` (`NotifyPatientMessageTransport`: GOV.UK Notify's REST API with a JWT made in code, one template each with `((message))` (and `((subject))`); `PatientMessages:Notify:ApiKey`, `SmsTemplateId`, `EmailTemplateId`, checked at startup). Its named HttpClient (`notify`) has no resilience handler (the API removes the service defaults' one): Notify has no idempotency key, so nothing retries a POST, and a timeout is `Unconfirmed` (shown as such, never sent again). The API key is a secret: never in the repository. The chart sets no mode, so kind and Azure are Off (`docs/todo.md`).
  - **The job** (`PatientRemindersJob`, `patient-reminders`, every 15 minutes, under the lease; [Scheduled jobs](jobs.md)): makes reminders from 09:00 to 19:00 UK for Booked appointments more than 2 hours and at most `Reminders:DaysBefore` (2, 1 to 14) days away, records open; with `Reminders:MissedMessage` (off by default) makes a missed-appointment message from 10:00 on the next working day (Monday to Friday, not closed everywhere) after a Did not attend, that day only; sends what's waiting (moves and cancellations at once, the rest 09:00 to 19:00), each checked again first against the appointment and patient read just before it (`PatientMessageRules.WhyNotToSend`: still as the message says (its time and room), still to come, the record open (a cancellation still goes to a restricted record), the channel still chosen) and sent to the contact on the record then, each saved as it goes; gives one up after 5 failed tries (`TooManyAttempts`), and stops the run's sending on a 429; asks about delivery for 3 days (no inbound webhook). Counts only in the log.
  - **Telling the patient of a change:** `POST .../book` (`tellPatient`, a Booked one moved to another time or room), `POST .../cancel` (`tellPatient`, a Booked one still to come) and `POST api/clinics/sessions/{id}/cancel` (`tellPatients`) stage the messages on the request's DbContext (`IPatientMessageOutbox`, `IPatientMessageRepository.Stage`), so they're saved with the change; the job sends them. The dialogs offer it only when `GET api/appointments/patient-messages/status` says messages are on, ticked when the patient has a choice.
  - **Reading them:** `GET api/appointments/{id}/messages` (`ViewPatients`, records `PatientViewed`), newest first. Exported (every message, with the reminder choice) and anonymised (destination and text replaced, waiting ones cancelled) through `PatientRecordRepository`.
- **The Did not attend list** (decision 132, hazard H-55): `GET api/appointments/search?view=didNotAttend` (`AppointmentSearch.AwaitingFollowUp`) lists appointments marked Did not attend with no later appointment for the same stage (else the same pathway, else the patient's appointments not for a pathway; not cancelled or entered in error, starting after it, or asked for after it) and no `NoFurtherAppointment` decision, each with `missedOnPathwayCount` (`IAppointmentRepository.CountMissedAsync`). Rebook and Clinician to review stay on it until the later appointment exists. Requires Appointment's **Did not attend** view shows it, oldest first.
  - **The follow-up:** `POST api/appointments/{id}/dna-follow-up` `{decision, detail?}` (`BookAppointments`): `DnaFollowUpDecision` (`Rebook = 0`, `ClinicianToReview = 1`, `NoFurtherAppointment = 2`), with a detail (500, free text, required for No further appointment), on the appointment (`DnaFollowUp`, `DnaFollowUpDetail`, when and by whom) and in its history (`DnaFollowUpRecorded = 9`, the decision by name and the detail, which the staff timeline leaves out). Only a Did not attend (409), not a restricted or anonymised record (409). Correcting the outcome to Attended clears it. **MedApp never discharges, re-prioritises or changes a pathway because of a missed appointment.**
- **Notifications** ([Notifications](notifications.md)) go to the member of staff, only when someone else made the change: `AppointmentBooked` (booked with you, or moved to you), `AppointmentRescheduled` (moved to another time), `AppointmentCancelled` (cancelled, or moved to someone else) and `AppointmentRecorded` (recorded after the event). Fixed titles; the message says who and when, never the patient or the notes. `Notification.AppointmentId` opens it on the Calendar page.
- **Deleting:** a patient with appointments can't be deleted (409). Deleting a pathway (only once cancelled or archived) detaches its appointments (pathway and stage set to none) in the same save; their history keeps it. An account with upcoming Booked appointments can't be deleted, or lose every staff role (`SetUserRolesHandler`), until they're moved or cancelled: both ask `WhyCantStopBeingStaffAsync` (`AppointmentRepositoryExtensions`) and refuse with `HasUpcomingAppointments`.
- **Privacy** ([Activity log and patient privacy](privacy.md)):
  - **Restriction:** changes load through `GetForUpdateOrThrowAsync` (`AppointmentRepositoryExtensions`, `PatientRules.EnsureCanChange`), and adding one checks the patient the same way. Cancelling uses `GetForCancelOrThrowAsync` (`PatientRules.EnsureCanPause`): allowed for a restricted patient, never an anonymised one.
  - **Reads:** opening an appointment records `PatientViewed`; the calendar and appointment search record `PatientListed` for the patients shown. `GET api/calendar?withoutAppointments=true` (Apply Template's days) records nothing.
  - **Export and anonymisation:** `PatientRecordRepository` loads a patient's appointments, their history and the notifications about them. `PatientAnonymiser` replaces appointments' notes and cancellation reasons, and the history's notes, reasons and pathway titles. Appointment notifications stay as they are (`Notification.HasFixedTitle`).
  - **Not monitored:** appointments aren't `MonitoringEvent`s, nor is the admin bypass yet (`docs/todo.md`).
- **Endpoints** are under `api/calendar` and `api/appointments` (the plan's section 4), closed days at `api/calendar/closed-days`. `GET api/calendar/staff` lists everyone with a role; `?permission=<name>` only the holders of one permission (see [Auth](auth.md)).
