# Referrals

Referrals, their history and their letters: read before changing any of them.

Concept and decisions: `docs/centre-phase2-plan.md` (decisions 8 to 25 and 43 to 50, section 9's PRs 5 and 6, and section 10's later decisions). A referral is **an administrative record** of one request (usually from a GP practice) for the centre to see one patient in one specialty, typed by staff from the letter. **The clinician decides; the app records:** nothing is scored, suggested or ordered by clinical content (`docs/clinical-safety/intended-purpose-and-device-decision.md`).
- **A referral** (`Referral`, `MedApp.Domain/Referrals`; table `Referrals`, migration `AddedReferrals`): the patient and the specialty asked for (both foreign keys, restrict), `ReceivedOn` (a UK date, not in the future, at most 365 days back), the referring practice (free text, 200, required), the referring clinician (free text, 200, optional: a third party's personal data), the practice's ODS code (optional), the priority asked for (`PatientPathwayPriority`), the reason (free text, 2,000, clinical), the status, who registered it and when (user ID and username), the decision columns, `PathwayId`, `Version` and a row version.
  - **The decision columns** (`DecidedAtUtc`/`ByUserId`/`ByUsername`, `RejectionReason`, `DecisionDetail` (500), `RedirectedTo` (200)) are filled by triage, withdrawing and entering in error. `RedirectDetail` (500) is why it was redirected here from another specialty (the latest such move's only), apart from `DecisionDetail` because it still awaits triage.
  - **ODS codes** (`OdsCodes`): 3 to 10 letters and digits once spaces are removed, stored upper case, **format only** (MedApp has no link to the ODS).
  - **The practice from the directory** (`PracticeId`, PR 12): optional beside the typed practice, on register and edit (`practiceId`; null for none). A newly chosen one must exist and not be archived; an empty typed practice or ODS code takes the directory's (`ReferralPractices`), and typed words are kept as typed. An edit that changes it records the field `directoryPractice` with the old and new IDs (never the name). Anonymising clears it. Referrals from before the directory have none.
- **Where the rules live** (`MedApp.Application/Referrals/Common`):
  - `ReferralWorkflow`: open and closed statuses (only `AwaitingTriage` is open), what can be done in each, and every message (`ChangedMeanwhileMessage`, `RestrictedRegisterMessage`, `PossibleDuplicateMessage`, `AlreadyTriagedMessage`, `NoTriagePermissionMessage`, `NotInSpecialtyMessage`, ...). `WhyCantEdit`, `WhyCantClose`, `WhyCantTriage`, `EnsureCanTriage`, `WhyFilesCantChange`, `ReadOnlyReason`.
  - `ReferralChange`: **the only way to change a referral.** It applies the change, bumps `Version` and writes its `ReferralEvent`s, which the repository saves in the same `SaveChangesAsync`.
  - `ReferralRules` (the validators' shared checks), `ReferralMapper` (with `ReferralResponses`, which asks the viewer's membership of its specialty; flags `CanEdit`, `CanClose`, `CanAttachFiles`, `CanTriage`, `WhyCantTriage`, `ReadOnlyReason`; `ToPathwayReferral` for the pathway's page), `ReferralRepositoryExtensions` (`GetForEditOrThrowAsync`, `GetForCloseOrThrowAsync`, `GetForTriageOrThrowAsync`, `TriageConflictAsync`, `GetOrThrowAsync`), `ReferralNotifications` (`ReferralTriaged`).
  - `ReferralSearch` (`Queries/SearchReferrals`): LINQ that runs in SQL and in memory, ending in `ThenBy(r => r.Id)`.
  - `IReferralRepository` (`Referrals/Repositories`): shares the request's DbContext with the pathway repository, so triage can save the referral and its new pathway together.
- **Endpoints** (`ReferralsController`, `api/referrals`):
  - `POST api/referrals` (`RegisterReferrals`; 201) `{patientId, specialtyId, receivedOn, referrerPractice, referrerClinician?, referrerOdsCode?, requestedPriority, reason, confirmPossibleDuplicate}`.
  - `GET api/referrals/search` (`ViewPatients`; a `PagedResponse`): `statuses`, `specialtyIds`, `mine` (the viewer's specialties; every specialty if they have none), `patientId`, `search` (the patient's name), `sort` (`receivedOn`, the default, `patient`, `specialty`, `priority`, `registered`) and `ascending`.
  - `GET api/referrals/awaiting-triage/count` (`ViewPatients`): the side nav's count, in the viewer's specialties (everyone's if they have none). **Records nothing:** a count names nobody.
  - `GET api/referrals/{id}` (`ViewPatients`): the referral with its history, letter count, flags and the patient's banner details and flags.
  - `PUT api/referrals/{id}` (`RegisterReferrals`): edit while awaiting triage, with `expectedVersion`.
  - `POST api/referrals/{id}/withdraw` and `…/entered-in-error` (`RegisterReferrals`) `{detail, expectedVersion}`: awaiting triage only; the detail is required (500).
  - **Triage** (`TriageReferrals` **and** membership of the referral's specialty, checked in the handler: 403 otherwise, `ReferralWorkflow.EnsureCanTriage`; the built-in Admin role doesn't grant it), each with `expectedVersion`:
    - `POST api/referrals/{id}/accept` `{templateId, priority, dueDate, title?, assigneeUserId?, notes?}`: 201 with the new pathway, `Location` its URL. The template must be the referral's specialty's (400 otherwise), not archived and with stages (409). The pathway is made by the same code as any new one (`PathwayCreation.FromTemplate` and `Record`, whose Created audit entry gains `referralId`; its first note and first stage's visit), and `IReferralRepository.AddEvents` puts the referral's `Accepted` entry in the context so `IPatientPathwayRepository.AddAsync` saves the pathway, its audit and the referral in **one `SaveChangesAsync`**. Then the assignee (if any) and the registrant are told, then the monitor runs. Assigning needs `AssignPathways` too (403), and the person must hold `WorkPathways` (400). **`dueDate` is the clinician's** until the waiting-time clock sets it (PR 7).
    - `POST api/referrals/{id}/reject` `{reason, detail?}`: a `ReferralRejectionReason` by name; the detail (500) is required for `Other`.
    - `POST api/referrals/{id}/redirect` `{specialtyId?, elsewhere?, detail}`: exactly one of the two, with a detail (500). To another specialty here (not its own: 400; not archived: 409) it stays awaiting triage, from the same date received, in that inbox (`SpecialtyChanged`, the detail in `RedirectDetail`); elsewhere (200 characters) it closes as `RedirectedElsewhere`.
    - **Nothing is prefilled or suggested** (decision 50): the web form's template, priority and target date start empty; templates are only the specialty's, by name.
    - **Two triagers at once:** the row version refuses the second save, and `TriageConflictAsync` reads the referral again to say who decided and how (409, `AlreadyTriagedMessage`); one that finds it decided already gets the same. A losing acceptance leaves no pathway (one save).
    - **Restricted or anonymised:** triage is refused (409), redirecting too.
- **Versions:** every change names `expectedVersion`; another change since is a 409 (`ReferralWorkflow.ChangedMeanwhileMessage`), and the row version catches two saves at once.
- **Duplicates are shown, never merged** (hazard H-44): registering looks for the patient's open referrals to the same specialty (awaiting triage, or accepted with the pathway still open); without `ConfirmPossibleDuplicate` it's a 400 on that field, naming when each was received and its status, **never its text** (`PossibleDuplicateMessage`).
- **Editing** while awaiting triage may change the specialty (decision 13's correction): the event records `DetailsEdited` with the specialty's old and new name. `SpecialtyChanged` is kept for triage's redirect.
- **History** (`ReferralEvent`, table `ReferralEvents`): no foreign keys; **structured values only** (statuses, the specialty's ID and name, the date received, the priority, the names of the fields edited), **never free text**: the reason, the referrer and the details live only on the referral, so an edit keeps no old text. Referral changes are recorded here, **not also in the activity log**.
- **Stored enums, never renumbered:** `ReferralStatus` (`AwaitingTriage = 0`, `Accepted = 1`, `Rejected = 2`, `RedirectedElsewhere = 3`, `Withdrawn = 4`, `EnteredInError = 5`), `ReferralRejectionReason` (`NotOfferedHere = 0`, `DoesNotMeetCriteria = 1`, `InsufficientInformation = 2`, `Duplicate = 3`, `Other = 4`) and `ReferralEventAction` (`Registered = 0` to `TargetChanged = 11`, all declared; the clock's 8 to 11 aren't written yet). `ActivityAction.ReferralViewed = 43`; `NotificationKind.ReferralTriaged = 23`.
- **Restricted and anonymised records** ([privacy](privacy.md)): registering, editing and attaching are refused (409). The restricted message tells staff to give the letter to an admin now, so it isn't lost (hazard H-40). **Withdrawing and marking entered in error stay allowed while restricted** (so care can be stopped safely), and are refused once anonymised (`WhyCantClose`). Restricting a patient counts their referrals awaiting triage in the confirmation (`ConfirmOpenPathways`).
- **Privacy:**
  - **Reads:** opening a referral records `ReferralViewed` against the patient with `{referralId}` in the details, through `IActivityLog.RecordViewAsync` (deduplicated per person and referral over the view window; fails closed). The search records `PatientListed` for each patient shown, and a search by the patient's name `PatientsSearched` with the names of the filters used (`ReferralSearch.FiltersUsed`, `via: "referrals"`), never the term. Every GET is classified in `EndpointCoverageTests`.
  - **Free text** (the reason, the referrer, the details, where it was redirected) never reaches a staff timeline, a notification, an email or a log line. Staff with `ViewPatients` read it on the referral page.
  - **Export and anonymisation:** `PatientRecordRepository` loads referrals and their history. The export gives `referrals` (every field, staff by username) and `referralHistory` (without staff IDs); letters show as attached to the referral. Anonymising is **refused while any referral awaits triage**; it replaces the reason, practice, clinician, decision and redirect details and redirect destination with placeholders and clears the ODS code (dates, statuses, priority and specialty stay); history details are scrubbed as others are.
  - **Deleting:** a patient with any referral can't be deleted (409). Deleting a pathway clears its referral's `PathwayId` in the same save.
  - **Retention** (`RetentionSchedule`): the referral history counts towards the conclusion of treatment (`LastReferralChangeUtc`), and a patient with a referral awaiting triage isn't a retention candidate.
- **The letter** is an attachment with a fourth target, `ReferralId` ([attachments](attachments.md)): `?referralId=` on list and upload, every attachment rule, listed with the patient's files. Not on a referral entered in error (`WhyFilesCantChange`); in any other status, since a letter can arrive after triage.
- **Timeline** ([timeline](timeline.md)): a third feed, kind `Referrals`, actions `Referral<Action>` (`ReferralRegistered`, ...). The overview lists referrals awaiting triage (`awaitingReferrals`).
- **Specialties** ([specialties](specialties.md)): a specialty with referrals is in use (archived, never deleted); archiving one with referrals awaiting triage is a 409.
- **Notified:** whoever registered a referral is told of every triage outcome (`ReferralTriaged = 23`, `ReferralNotifications`), unless they triaged it: a fixed title, "Referral triaged", and "k.ashford accepted a Dermatology referral." (rejected; redirected elsewhere; "redirected a Dermatology referral to Urology."). It carries `ReferralId`, which the export finds it by. No email, and nothing for a new referral (the inbox count is the signal).
- **The web app:** the Referrals page (`/referrals`: Awaiting triage, oldest received first; Closed; All; a **Specialty** filter, `specialty` in the URL, any of, sent as `specialtyIds`; it doesn't yet start on the viewer's specialties with `mine`), its side nav item with the awaiting count, Register Referral (`/referrals/new`: find or add the patient, duplicates shown inline), the referral page (banner, details, letter, the patient's other referrals, history; edit, withdraw and entered in error; **Accept**, **Reject** and **Redirect** for triagers, or why the viewer can't; each with a confirmation showing the patient banner, the specialty and the choice), the patient page's Referrals card, the pathway page's link to its referral, the timeline's entries and the export's section. Its actions follow the response's flags.
- **The pathway's referral:** `GET api/pathways/patient-pathways/{id}` gives `referral` (ID, date received, specialty), read by `IReferralRepository.GetForPathwayAsync`; accepting fills it too, other pathway responses leave it null (the web app keeps the cached one).
- **Not built yet:** the waiting-time clock (PR 7), and demo data (PR 9).
