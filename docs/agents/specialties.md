# Specialties

The centre's specialties (its services), each template's and pathway's specialty, and staff memberships: read before changing any of them.

Concept and decisions: `docs/centre-phase2-plan.md` (decisions 1 to 7, and section 9's PR 1). Specialties are **organisational labels**: they decide nothing clinical, and membership changes nothing about what anyone can see (decision 67).
- **A specialty** (`Specialty`, `MedApp.Domain/Specialties`) is a name (unique ignoring case, 100 characters), archived or not, with a `Version`. The rules and messages are in `MedApp.Application/Specialties/Common` (`SpecialtyRules`, `SpecialtyMapper`, `SpecialtyUsage`); the repository is `ISpecialtyRepository`.
  - **Endpoints:** `GET api/specialties` (`includeArchived`) and `GET api/specialties/{id}`, `UseApp` (anyone with a role); `POST`, `PUT api/specialties/{id}` `{name, expectedVersion}`, `POST …/{id}/archive` and `…/restore` `{expectedVersion}`, and `DELETE …/{id}`, `ManageSetup`. Each response says how many templates, pathways, referrals and members of staff have it, and `CanBeDeleted`.
  - **Versions:** every change names the version the caller saw; another admin's change since is a 409 (`SpecialtyRules.ChangedMeanwhileMessage`). `Version` is also a concurrency token: the repository's update sets its original value to the version read, so two saves at once can't both win.
  - **In use, archived, never deleted:** a specialty any template, pathway, referral or member of staff has can't be deleted (409 naming what has it; the foreign keys refuse too, if something took it meanwhile). An archived one can't be chosen for a template or newly given to anyone (409, `SpecialtyRules.CheckChosenAsync`); whatever already has it keeps it. **Archiving one with referrals awaiting triage is a 409** (`SpecialtyRules.AwaitingTriageMessage`, saying how many), so none is stranded (hazard H-40). `SpecialtyUsage` counts its referrals (`ReferralCount`) and those awaiting triage (`AwaitingTriageCount`).
  - **General** (`Specialty.GeneralId`, seeded through `HasData`, like the default stage definition): migration `AddedSpecialties` put every existing template and pathway in it. It's the database's default for both `SpecialtyId` columns, so an API copy from before specialties still creates them (in General) during a rollout. Admins may rename, archive or (once nothing has it) delete it.
- **Every pathway template belongs to one** (`PathwayTemplate.SpecialtyId`, required on create: 400 without; `PATCH` takes an optional `specialtyId` to move it). The response gives `specialtyId` and `specialtyName`.
- **A pathway copies its template's** when it's created (`PathwayCreation.FromTemplate` sets `PatientPathway.SpecialtyId`), as it copies the stages; moving the template later moves only new pathways. The pathway's response gives `specialtyId`. Lists and Insights filter on the pathway's own copy: the pathway search's `SpecialtyIds` (any of; the list's and My Worklist's `specialty` filter) and Insights' `SpecialtyId` (`InsightsScope`); see [Pathways](pathways.md) and [Insights](insights.md).
- **Staff belong to any number** (`StaffSpecialty`, keyed by user and specialty; deleted with the account by the foreign key's cascade). `GET`/`PUT api/auth/users/{username}/specialties` `{specialtyIds}` (`ManageUsers`, as the plan's decision 68 puts specialty membership; the PUT sets exactly that set and returns it) and `GET api/auth/me/specialties` (`UseApp`, their own). Handlers: `SetUserSpecialties`, `GetUserSpecialties`, `GetMySpecialties` under `MedApp.Application/Authentication/Users`.
  - **Logged:** a change records `StaffSpecialtiesChanged = 42` in the activity log (the account as target, the specialties' names and IDs before and after), saved in the same `SaveChangesAsync` as the memberships (`ISpecialtyRepository.SetForUserAsync`). Setting what someone already has records nothing.
  - **Triage** needs the `TriageReferrals` permission **and** membership of the referral's specialty ([referrals](referrals.md)). Membership alone grants nothing.
  - **Who can triage each:** `SpecialtyResponse.TriagerCount` counts its members who hold `TriageReferrals` (`SpecialtyUsage.TriagerCount`, through `RolePermissionQueries.HoldersOf`); the Specialties page warns about any that isn't archived and has none (hazard H-40).
- **Not yet:** waiting-time targets on specialties (the clock's PR adds them), and specialties on calendars and clinics (Phase 4).
- **The web app:** the Specialties page (`/admin/specialties`, under Admin after Users), the template editor's Specialty field, the Users page's Specialties dialog, and the read-only list in Account Settings.
- **Demo data:** `DemoSpecialties` (`tools/MedApp.DevMcp/DemoData`) makes Dermatology, Gynaecology, Urology, Diabetes and Biomechanics through the commands, puts each template in its own, and gives the clinicians theirs (the clinical lead, `admin`, every one); General stays, empty.
