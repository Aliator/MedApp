# Specimens

Specimen tracking, the lab's worklist and the specimen setup lists: read before changing any of them.

Concept and decisions: `docs/centre-phases-3-5-plan.md`, section 2.2 (decisions 81 to 90) and PR 13. A **specimen** (`Specimen`) is taken from a patient at a pathway's stage, sent to a lab and tracked there and back by its own number until the lab's report (a file) is attached. **MedApp tracks and shows specimens; it never reads, parses or interprets a report** (decision 69; `docs/clinical-safety/intended-purpose-and-device-decision.md`). Turnaround and "Overdue at lab" are date arithmetic, nothing clinical.
- **The rules live in `MedApp.Application/Specimens/Common`:** `SpecimenWorkflow` (statuses, what each step needs, who may mark one entered in error, overdue, times), `SpecimenChange` (applies a change, moves `Version`, writes the audit entries), `SpecimenNumbers`, `SpecimenMapper`, `SpecimenNotifications`, `SpecimenChoices` (type, lab and requesting clinician checks). Use them; don't set `Status` or the step fields by hand.
- **The number** is `{prefix}{yy}-{nnnnnn}` (`MA26-000123`): the prefix from `Specimens:NumberPrefix` (one to four capitals, default `MA`; checked at startup, `SpecimenOptions`), the UK year it was recorded, and the next value of the SQL sequence `SpecimenNumbers` (`ISpecimenRepository.NextNumberAsync`, `SELECT NEXT VALUE FOR`, never composed: SQL Server allows it only at the top of a SELECT), so API copies never clash. Unique (`IX_Specimens_Number`). Finding one by its number is `POST api/specimens/find {number}`: **the number stays out of URLs**, so out of request logs.
- **Statuses** (`SpecimenStatus`, stored by number, never renumber): `Taken = 0`, `Sent = 1`, `Received = 2`, `Reported = 3`, `RejectedByLab = 4`, `Lost = 5`, `EnteredInError = 6`. Forward only: Taken → Sent → Received → Reported; from Sent or Received, RejectedByLab (with `SpecimenRejectionReason`: `Unlabelled = 0`, `Mislabelled = 1`, `Leaked = 2`, `InsufficientSample = 3`, `WrongContainer = 4`, `Other = 5`, detail required for Other); from any open one, Lost or EnteredInError (detail required). Every closed status is final: another specimen is a new one (the page lists the stage's others).
- **Times:** taken, sent and received are now or typed up to 7 days back, never in the future, never before the step before (a 400 on the field); reported is now. `RecordedAtUtc` is when it was recorded (the 24-hour window below runs from it).
- **Who** (decision 84; permissions `RecordSpecimens` and `ProcessSpecimens`, group Lab, **not in Admin's fixed set**: they're clinical, decision 74): `RecordSpecimens` records taken (`POST api/pathways/{id}/specimens`), sent, and edits details before it's sent (`PUT api/specimens/{id}`); `ProcessSpecimens` receives, rejects and reports; either marks it lost (the endpoint needs `ViewPatients`, the handler checks: 403). **Entered in error:** whoever recorded it, while they hold `RecordSpecimens`, within 24 hours of recording it, or a holder of `CorrectRecords` at any time (handler check, 403). Every change carries `expectedVersion` (409 if stale; the row version catches two at once).
- **Taking needs** the stage to be editable (`PathwayWorkflow.WhyStageCantBeEdited`: the current one, or one an admin unlocked) and the pathway open (not on hold or closed): `SpecimenWorkflow.WhyCantTake`, also behind the stage's `CanRecordSpecimen` flag. The lab's steps work in any pathway status, so a report is never stuck.
- **The requesting clinician** must hold `SignOffResults` (`SpecimenChoices.RequestingClinicianPermission`): they get the result to sign off ([Results](results.md)); someone already named stays allowed.
- **Types** (`SpecimenType`: name, expected turnaround, 1 to 90 days, and a SNOMED CT code) are a setup list: read with `UseApp`, changed with `ManageSetup` (`api/specimen-types`); the external labs list was dropped (MC13, below). One a specimen names is archived, not deleted (409), and can't be chosen for a new specimen; a specimen keeps an archived one it already has.
- **The report** (`POST api/specimens/{id}/report?expectedVersion=`, the file as the body as an attachment upload is, `X-File-Name`) goes through `AttachmentIntake`, the same intake as every upload (sniffed type, size, malware scan, thumbnail), attached to the pathway, or the patient if the pathway no longer takes files. The file's record, its `AttachmentAdded` entry, the specimen's change and its `SpecimenReported` entry are one save (`SaveReportAsync`). **A specimen's report can't be deleted on its own** (`DeleteAttachmentHandler`: 409, admins too; decision 71). Reporting makes a result from it in the same save, and tells the requesting clinician ([Results](results.md)). **A wrong report** is corrected by marking its result entered in error, which deletes the file and puts the specimen back to Received, to be reported again.
- **The label and request sheet** are a printable page in the web app (`/specimens/{id}/print`), from the specimen's own GET (which records `SpecimenViewed`): the number as text and as a QR code drawn on the server by `IQrCodeRenderer` (the response's `label`: one SVG path), **holding the number only**, never patient details; the patient's name, date of birth and NHS number beside it (hazards H-01, H-47).
- **The lab's worklist** (`GET api/specimens/search?view=`, `SpecimenSearch`, LINQ that runs in SQL and in tests): `ToSend`, `Expected`, `InLab`, `Overdue` (open past taken + the type's days: `DATEADD` in SQL), `RejectedAndLost` (30 days), `Reported` (7 days), oldest first; `All` (the default) newest taken first, with `pathwayId`, `patientId` and `laboratoryId` filters. Every list ends on the ID. **The Lab page** uses `POST api/specimens/search` (`SpecimenWorklistRequest`, same handler and `PatientListed`): the view and lab, plus `search` (the number without spaces, the patient's name or NHS number, the type) and `sort` (`number`, `patient`, `lab`, `status`, `time`: the view's time) with `ascending`. A POST because the search may be a specimen number, which stays out of URLs; the page keeps it in its own state, never its address. "Find by specimen number" is gone from the page: a number typed or scanned into its search just narrows the list (`POST api/specimens/find` stays, unused by the page).
- **The last stage's gates** (decisions 89 and 95): `StageGates.WhyCantFinish` (`MedApp.Application/Pathways/Common`, the home of the gates without an override) refuses finishing the stage that would leave none unfinished while any of the pathway's specimens is open (409 naming them), and `StageGates.WhyResultsAwaiting` while any of its results awaits sign-off ([Results](results.md)). Every finish path calls it: `FinishPathwayStageHandler` and the demo's `PathwayReplay`.
- **Audit** (`PatientPathwayAuditAction` 16 to 23: `SpecimenTaken`, `SpecimenSent`, `SpecimenReceived`, `SpecimenReported`, `SpecimenRejected`, `SpecimenLost`, `SpecimenEnteredInError`, `SpecimenDetailsEdited`), written by `SpecimenChange`, not `PathwayChange` (a specimen isn't a change to the pathway; its `LastUpdated` doesn't move). **Details hold the specimen's ID and number, type and lab by ID and name, statuses, the rejection reason by name, times and an edit's field names (values for type, lab, taken time and requesting clinician only); never the site, request notes, references or any detail** (DPIA risk 29).
- **Notifications** `SpecimenRejectedByLab = 24` ("Specimen rejected", "The lab rejected a Skin biopsy (punch) specimen.") and `SpecimenLost = 25` ("Specimen lost", "k.ashford marked a … specimen lost.") go to the requesting clinician and whoever took it, never the actor, with `SubjectId` the specimen (the web app links to its page) and `PathwayId`. Fixed titles (`Notification.HasFixedTitle`).
- **Privacy** ([privacy.md](privacy.md)): opening a specimen (its page, label, request sheet, or finding it by number) records `SpecimenViewed = 45` with `{specimenId}`; the worklist records `PatientListed`. A restricted record (decision 75): no new specimen and no edits (409, "ask an admin"); a taken one can still be sent, received, rejected, reported, marked lost or entered in error. Anonymised: nothing. The export lists specimens (`ExportedSpecimenResponse`, staff by username); anonymisation waits while one is open, then replaces the site, request notes and closing detail and removes the lab and courier references (numbers, statuses, dates, types and labs stay); the report is deleted with every file. A pathway with specimens can't be deleted (409; the foreign key too).
- **Timeline** ([timeline.md](timeline.md)): kind `Specimens`. Staff see the action, who, when, the number, type, lab and statuses, and an edit's structured values; admins also the site and request notes (on Taken) and the closing detail and rejection reason, read from the specimen (`PatientTimelineRow.Specimen`). `TimelineStaffCanaryTests` covers every specimen action.

## The lab: tests, samples, cases and clocks (MC13 to MC19)

Screens say **sample**; the code keeps `Specimen`. `docs/multi-clinic-plan.md`, section 5; the light demo's `labCases.ts`, `labClock.ts`, `lab.ts`, `labTests.ts`.
- **The test catalogue** (`LabTest`, `api/lab-tests`, read `UseApp`, changed `ManageSetup`; `MedApp.Application/Lab/Tests`): name, `Kind`, SNOMED CT code (6 to 18 digits), `TargetDays`/`LimitDays` (1 to 90, target ≤ limit), `AtRiskDays` (0 to 30, under the target), 1 to 20 ordered steps (`LabStepKind`; optional ones for "Needs more work?"). A step kept in its place keeps its ID. One a case or sample used is archived, not deleted (409). Specimen types gain `SnomedCode`. **The external labs list (`Laboratory`, `api/laboratories`) is gone** (MC13): specimen audit entries name the test and source; older ones keep `laboratory`, as written.
- **Samples (MC14):** `Source` (`SpecimenSource`), `RequestingTeamId` (the clinic), `LabTestId` (optional, never chosen for the clinician), `LabCaseId`, `HospitalId`/`HospitalRequestId` (MC18, no foreign keys yet); `PathwayId` and `StageId` nullable. **Walls** (`LabWalls`): the lab team and admins see every sample and case; a clinic its own (team, or a pathway it sees); a hospital's only the lab; elsewhere the recorded 404. Every specimen command and read loads through them; `RecordSpecimen` needs the pathway seen. Search (`SpecimenSearch`): `ToReceive` (taken or sent) plus the old views, and `source`, `teamId`, `labTestId`, `status`, `due` (`Overdue`, `DueSoon`, `NotDue`); what follows a case's clock (the Overdue view, Due, sorting by due) is worked out in memory over the open samples SQL narrowed. A sample in a case can't be reported on its own (409): its case is.
- **Lab cases (MC15, `LabCase`, `LabCaseStep`, `api/lab-cases`; `MedApp.Application/Lab`):** numbered from the specimen sequence. Work runs Received, Accessioned, "Sample adequate?" (`Adequate`/`Insufficient`), the test's required steps, "Needs more work?" (`NoMoreWork`, `ExtraTests`, `SecondOpinion`: the extra two only when the test has those optional steps, which they add before it's asked again) (`LabCaseRules.Next`), then the results. **Only the lab team works a case** (`ProcessSpecimens` and membership: 409 `LabWorkMessage`), from `expectedVersion`; only what's next (409 `NotNext`). Outcomes are the lab member's, never suggested. **Authorising** (`AuthoriseLabReports`, Pathologist) takes the results as text (4,000; the report as a file is a later step), reports the samples, makes a clinic case's `Result` (`LabCaseId`, `ReportText`, no file: `ReportAttachmentId` empty) for the requesting clinician (`ResultReady`). **Insufficient** (why required) closes it `RepeatRequested`, rejects its samples (insufficient), reopens the stage that took them (`PathwayChange.ReopenForRepeatSample`) and tells whoever asked and took it (`RepeatSampleRequested = 46`, a fixed title). Only the latest step can be entered in error, by whoever recorded it (403); a case by its opener within 24 hours or `CorrectRecords`, freeing its samples. **`LabCaseChange` is the only way to change a case**: audit `LabCaseOpened = 50` to `LabCaseEnteredInError = 55` (IDs, numbers, names, outcomes; never the results or a detail), shown on the timeline with the samples. Reading one records `LabCaseViewed = 55` with `access`. Receiving a clinic sample with a test opens its case or joins its stage's (`LabReceipt`); without one the lab opens it by hand (`POST api/lab-cases`).
- **Lab stages (MC16):** reaching a lab-owned stage opens its case, waiting for its sample (`PatientPathwayRepository`, in the change's save); the first sample received joins it and picks the stage up; authorising puts the results on the stage (`PutLabResults`: starts and picks it up, never finishes it); the lab finishes the stage by hand, and while its case is open that's a 409 (`StageGates.WhyLabCaseOpen`). The stage response's `labCaseId`, `labCaseNumber`, `labCaseStatus` name its open case, else its latest. The lab's pathway loads use `GetForLabWorkOrThrowAsync` (allowed while restricted, not anonymised). A pathway or patient with lab cases can't be deleted (409).
- **Clocks (MC19, `LabClock`):** received + the test's days in UK dates (a hospital's date with MC18: the sooner applies, a tie names the hospital), at risk from `AtRiskDays` before, breached after; `clock` on cases, summaries and samples. Views `Open`, `AtRisk`, `Overdue`, `ToReport` are ordered in memory (open cases only). Service alerts `LabCaseAtRisk = 16`, `LabCaseOverdue = 17` (`MonitoredLabCaseAtRisk = 47`, `MonitoredLabCaseOverdue = 48`) through the `service-alerts` job; no template or priority (400), their team the clinic it came from.
- **Privacy:** cases are in the export (`ExportedLabCaseResponse`, with the stage's `ResultText` and the result's `ReportText`), anonymised (the free text replaced; refused while a case is open). A patient known only through a hospital case is hidden from non-lab staff's search (`PatientSearch.HospitalOnlyHidden`).
- **Stored, never renumbered:** `LabTestKind`, `LabStepKind`, `SpecimenSource`, `LabCaseStatus`, `LabCaseStepKind` (`Received = 0`, `Accessioned = 1`, `TestStep = 2`, `Outcome = 3`), `LabChoice` (`SampleAdequate = 0`, `NeedsMoreWork = 1`), `LabOutcome` (`Adequate = 0`, `Insufficient = 1`, `NoMoreWork = 2`, `ExtraTests = 3`, `SecondOpinion = 4`).
