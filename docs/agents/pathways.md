# Pathways

Pathways, templates and stages, pathway notes and note deletion: read before changing any of them.

Concept and decisions: `docs/pathways-ui-plan.md`. Admins build **templates**, ordered lists of **stage definitions** (a stage may repeat). Staff create a patient's **pathway** from one, copying its stages (name, description, instructions). Stages are worked strictly in order: notes, then finish, which locks the stage until a holder of `UnlockStages` (Admin, Clinician, Senior clinician to start with) unlocks it.
- **The rules live in `MedApp.Application/Pathways/Common`:** `PathwayWorkflow` (statuses, current stage, what's editable, overdue) and `PathwayChange` (applies a change, moves the status, writes the audit entries). Use them; don't set `Status` or stage flags by hand.
- **Every change is audited** (`PatientPathwayAuditEntry`, no foreign keys so history outlives deletes). New pathway commands must go through `PathwayChange`.
- **Target dates are whole days,** due by the end of the day UK time, stored as that moment in UTC (`DueDates`); the API takes and returns `DueDate` as a date.
- **A pathway accepted from a referral takes its target date from the waiting-time clock** while the clock runs (its target) or is paused (the review date); `PATCH` refuses a different one then (400, `WaitingClock.TargetFromClockMessage`), and staff edit it again once the clock has stopped. The clock stops when the stage marked `StopsClock` (copied from the template's clock-stop stage) is finished, or the pathway is discharged or cancelled; a new priority re-reads its target. All through `PathwayChange` (`FollowClock`, `PriorityChanged`, `PauseClock`, `ResumeClock`), which loads the referral with the pathway: see [Referrals](referrals.md), the waiting-time clock.
- **Every template belongs to a team,** and a pathway copies its template's when it's created: see [Teams](teams.md).
- **The team walls** ([Teams](teams.md)): a pathway is read only by its team, a team it was passed to (the lab) and admins; everyone else gets a 404 (recorded) and sees it only as a summary on the patient. **A command loads it with the boundary and the actor's scope** (`GetForUpdateOrThrowAsync(id, boundary, request.Actor.Teams, ct)`): only its own team changes it (409 for the lab or an admin). Lists and labels filter by the viewer's scope; staff start pathways only from their teams' templates.
- **A pathway can come from a referral:** accepting one creates it through `PathwayCreation` like any new pathway, with `referralId` in its Created audit entry, saved with the referral's change: see [Referrals](referrals.md). Its page links back (`PatientPathwayResponse.Referral`).
- **List filters** (`PathwaySearch`, `GET api/pathways/patient-pathways/search`; the Pathways list and My Worklist): besides the rest, `TeamIds` (any of the pathways' own teams) and **the stage reached**, `StageDefinitionIds` (`PathwaySearch.AtStage`): pathways one of whose current stages (through the stage links, as `PathwayWorkflow.CurrentStages` has them) comes from any of those definitions. Closed pathways (discharged, cancelled, archived) never match, whatever else is asked; on hold ones do. At most 100 IDs each. `BreachedOnly` keeps pathways whose referral's clock is past its target, and `sort=daysWaited` orders by the clock's days waited (pathways without one last, either way; `DateOnly.DayNumber`, which EF turns into `DATEDIFF`: `WaitingClockSqlTests`). The web app keeps them in the URL as `team` and `stage` (comma lists of IDs, "is" or "is any of", never "not any of": `parseIdChoice`). `View` (MC7: `OurTurn`, `NewForUs`, `AwaitingLab`, `BackFromLab`, for the viewer's teams, open pathways only; anything else a 400) and the clinic picker's `TeamId` (MC6: one of the viewer's own teams unless they see every team, else a 400). `PathwaySearchSqlTests` checks the SQL's shape; `PathwayFiltersTests` runs it on SQL Server.
- **Tree pathways (MC8), choice nodes (MC10b), optional stages** (`docs/multi-clinic-plan.md`; `docs/centre-phases-3-5-plan.md`, decisions 113 to 117). A pathway's stages are linked (`PatientPathwayStageLinks`, `PathwayGraph`): a stage is **current** when neither finished nor skipped and nothing before it on any route is still to do (`PathwayWorkflow.CurrentStages`; `CurrentStage` is the first); forks run side by side, a join waits for each route. A pathway without links (from before them) is a chain by `StageOrder`; adding a branch writes its chain first. In SQL a current stage is one whose direct predecessors are all done. A skipped stage counts as done for the status (`AllStagesFinished`). Responses: `currentStageIds`, each stage's `predecessorIds`; summaries name every current stage ("A and B").
  - **On the template** (`PathwayTemplateMap.IsOptional`, `ChoiceKind` (`StageChoiceKind` `Assessment = 0`, `LabResult = 1`) and `Outcomes` (`{name, nextStageOrders}`, JSON), `OwnerTeamId`; `PathwayTemplate.Links` and `AddableBranches`, JSON). The API takes `stageOptions` beside `stageDefinitionIdsInOrder`, and `links`. `TemplateShape` and `TemplateGraphRules` (the web's `templateGraphProblems`, word for word; 400s): links sent are checked ("Stage N: …" on `links`); a template with routes that sends none keeps them and can't change its stages as a list; a list is a chain (last stage not optional, no choice node). Options left out with the same stage list stay. A pathway keeps the template's whole tree as its `Plan` (JSON) when it's made.
  - **Skip** (`POST .../stages/{sid}/skip` `{reason, detail?}`, `WorkPathways`): an optional stage while it's current; one whose visit is **Required** only by a holder of `FinishWithoutVisit` (409 otherwise, and `canSkip` says so) with a detail (400), as finishing it without its visit needs, and its `StageSkipped` records `visitRequirement`; `StageSkipReason` by name, `NotNeeded`, `PatientDeclined` or `Other` (detail required, 500). Audit `StageSkipped` (`skipReason`, `detail`). An unstarted pathway goes InProgress.
  - **Choice nodes grow the pathway:** it's made with the stages up to its first choice node (`PathwayGrowth`, the web's `grownStageOrders`/`grownLinks`); finishing one for the first time needs the clinician's `outcome` (400 "Record the outcome: …", none pre-selected; any other finish refuses one), records it (`PatientPathwayStage.Outcome`) and adds that outcome's stages (renumbered, so `StageOrder` isn't unique), with one `BranchChosen` entry `{outcome, stageIds}`. Outcomes not chosen never appear. A choice node is never the last stage for the gates. Each stage lists its `outcomes` with the stages each would add, and `needsOutcome`.
  - **Reinstate** (`POST .../stages/{sid}/reinstate` `{reason}`, `UnlockStages`): a skipped stage, while the pathway is open or discharged (as unlocking); it's to do again in its place (before the current stage, it becomes current; its visit is asked for then). Audit `StageReinstated` (`reason`, the old `skipReason`). InReview or Completed goes back to InProgress.
  - **Visits:** a skipped stage has none. Skipping cancels its Requested appointments (`PathwayChange.StageSkippedReason`); Booked ones stay, as when a stage is finished. A finish or skip loads the appointments of every stage still to do (`StageFinishing.StagesTouchedByFinish`) and asks for the visits of the stages it reached (`StageVisits.RequestForCurrentStage`).
  - **Elsewhere:** the stage filter's stage reached and Insights' stage times read `SkippedAtUtc` (below and [Insights](insights.md)); the timeline's Stages kind has the three actions ([Timeline](timeline.md)); the monitor is told of each command, though no rule watches skips ([Monitoring](monitoring.md)). **The waiting-time clock:** skipping the clock-stop stage (by hand or by a branch not chosen) stops nothing; the clock runs on and stops at discharge or cancellation, as with no clock-stop stage; reinstating never restarts a stopped clock, and a reinstated clock-stop stage stops a running one when it's finished (`PathwayChangeClockTests`).
  - **Stored:** `PatientPathwayAuditAction` `StageSkipped = 35`, `BranchChosen = 36`, `StageReinstated = 37` (16 to 34 kept for Phase 3), `StageHandedOver = 47`, `StagePickedUp = 48`, `BranchAdded = 49`; `StageSkipReason` `BranchNotChosen = 0` (no longer written), `NotNeeded = 1`, `PatientDeclined = 2`, `Other = 3`; `StageChoiceKind`; `NotificationKind.PathwayHandedOver = 44`. Never renumber them.
- **The relay (MC7):** a stage may be owned by the lab (`OwnerTeamId`; on the template only null, the template's team or the lab: 400 `OwnerMessage`). When a finish, skip, new pathway or added branch makes a stage current (`PathwayChange.ReachNext`) it's reached (`ReachedAtUtc`); if its team (`PathwayGraph.StageTeam`) differs from the finished stage's, it's **handed over**: `StageHandedOver` `{stageId, fromTeamId, from, toTeamId, to}`, the `StageOwner` grant (the repository writes it once), and the receiving team's members holding `WorkPathways` are told (`IPathwayHandovers`, `PathwayHandedOver`, "<username> passed a pathway to your team.") after the save. **Pick up:** `POST …/stages/{stageId}/pick-up` (`WorkPathways`; current, handed over, else 409; again saves nothing; `StagePickedUp`); starting or finishing it picks it up too, and `PathwayChange.PickUp` is there for the lab's first sample. **Only the stage's team works it** (`GetForStageWorkOrThrowAsync`, `TeamAccess.CanWorkStage`: 409 `StageNotYours` for the pathway's own team); the team working a current stage adds notes (`GetForNotesOrThrowAsync`); everything else stays the owner team's. A lab stage is finished by hand by the lab; `PathwayChange.PutLabResults` puts its case's results on it (`ResultText`, never audited) and `ReopenForRepeatSample` reopens the stage that took an insufficient sample (MC22); a lab stage's case and its gate: [Specimens](specimens.md), the lab.
- **Addable branches (MC10):** `POST …/patient-pathways/{id}/branches` `{templateBranchId, afterStageId, reason}` (`WorkPathways`, the pathway's own team; reason required, 500): only from the plan's branches, after a template stage it may follow (`PathwayBranches.AfterStagesFor`), once unless repeatable; its stages are a chain on their own route (`BranchAdditionId`), owned as the template owns the same definition. `BranchAdded` `{templateBranchId, branch, afterStageId, stageIds}`; the reason only on `PathwayBranchAdditions` (anonymised). Responses: `addableBranches` (never suggested), `canAddBranch`, `branchAdditions`.
- **The MDT list** (`MedApp.Application/Mdt`, `api/mdt`; plan decisions 141 to 144, PR 30): an `MdtItem` puts a pathway on a team's MDT (a calendar meeting with `IsMdt` and `TeamId`, set in the meeting dialog; a meeting request that leaves `isMdt` out keeps it as it is) with a question (1,000). `WorkPathways` lists (an open or on-hold pathway, an MDT that hasn't ended, once per meeting), defers (to a later MDT: Deferred; after a cancelled meeting, listed again as it was) and withdraws; `RecordMdtOutcomes` (Clinician and Senior clinician; not Admin's fixed set) records the outcome (2,000, as written) and who was present (from the meeting's attendees) once the meeting has started: Discussed, final. Every change carries `expectedVersion` and has a row version (`MdtRules`). **MedApp never suggests an outcome or changes the pathway because of one;** the pathway's assignees are told (`MdtOutcomeRecorded = 41`, a fixed title). Restricted: listing and deferring refused (409), withdrawing and the outcome allowed; anonymised: nothing. Audit `MdtListed = 38`, `MdtOutcomeRecorded = 39`, `MdtDeferred = 40`, `MdtWithdrawn = 41` (`MdtAudit`: IDs, team, meeting times, who was present; never the question or outcome; the timeline's `Mdt` kind). `MdtItemStatus`: `Listed = 0`, `Discussed = 1`, `Deferred = 2`, `Withdrawn = 3`. Cancelling an MDT clears its items' meeting (`MeetingCancelledStartUtc`: "Meeting cancelled: list again"); one with outcomes can't be cancelled, and one with items keeps its team. Deleting a pathway deletes its items. Reads: a meeting's list and the awaiting list `PatientListed`, a pathway's items `PathwayViewed`. **Team walls** (MC5, `MdtWalls`): lists and counts show only items whose pathway (or own team) the viewer sees; listing is the pathway's team's, deferring and withdrawing the item's (the MDT's) team's, the outcome whoever sees the item. **MC21 and MC26:** `POST api/mdt/items/{id}/presenter` (`WorkPathways`, the item's team only; one of the meeting's attendees, 400 otherwise, or null; `expectedVersion`; no audit entry), kept on a move only if invited to the new MDT; items carry `labWork` (the pathway's samples, with their results). **Every attendee, from any team, gets an `Mdt` grant per listed item** (`MdtGrants`, by person, read only, `PathwayAccess.MdtItemId`), synced in the same save as listing, a move, a withdrawal, an outcome and a meeting's attendee change, ended when an MDT is cancelled, and by the `mdt-grants` job once the item is discussed or withdrawn and the meeting has ended. Attendees from elsewhere get `readOnlyReason` on the item, the pathway (`TeamWalls.MdtReadOnly`) and the list (`MdtMeetingListResponse.readOnlyReason`).
- **Consults (MC25)** (`MedApp.Application/Consults`, `api/consults`): a pathway's own team sends another team (a clinic or the lab) a question (2,000); the asked team replies (4,000); the asking team closes it after the reply or withdraws it before one (`ConsultRules`; every change carries `expectedVersion`, with a row version). `WorkPathways` for every change, no new permission; one open consult per asked team per pathway (a filtered unique index too); restricted: sending and replying refused, closing and withdrawing allowed. **Walls:** the asked team's `Consult` grant is written with it and ended when it closes (`ConsultChange`, one save); its `readOnlyReason` says so (`TeamWalls.ConsultReadOnly`, from `PathwayMapper`'s `viewerAccess`); another team's consult is the recorded 404. `GET pathways/{pathwayId}` (`canRequest`; `PathwayViewed`) and `GET incoming` (`teamId`, the picker's rule; `PatientListed`). **Told:** `ConsultRequested = 49` to the asked team's members who work pathways, `ConsultReplied = 50` to the asker and the pathway's assignees (fixed titles, usernames and team names only). **Audit:** `ConsultRequested = 59` to `ConsultWithdrawn = 62` (`{consultId, askingTeamId, askingTeam, askedTeamId, askedTeam}`, never the text; the timeline leaves them out). `ConsultStatus`: `Open = 0`, `Replied = 1`, `Closed = 2`, `Withdrawn = 3`. Deleting a pathway deletes its consults; anonymising replaces their text and ends open ones. A consult meeting (`ConsultId` on meetings) isn't built: the contract has no field for it.
- **Definitions and templates in use are archived, not deleted** (deleting one is a 409). Patients with pathways or appointments can't be deleted (409).
- **A stage can involve a visit** (`VisitRequirement`, copied onto the stage): see [Calendar and appointments](calendar.md).
- **Stage gates without an override** live in `StageGates` (`Pathways/Common`), called by every finish path (`FinishPathwayStageHandler`, the demo's `PathwayReplay`): today the last stage (the one whose finish leaves none unfinished) waits while any of the pathway's specimens is open ([Specimens](specimens.md)). A stage's `CanRecordSpecimen` says whether the viewer can record one there.
- **Consent records** (PR 31, decisions 145 to 147; `MedApp.Application/Consent`): a stage definition's `NeedsConsent` (copied onto each pathway's stage, like the letter gate) stops it being finished without a **Valid** consent record for that stage (`StageGates.WhyConsentMissing`, 409, no override). A record (`ConsentRecord`): the stage, the form (`ConsentForm`, `ManageSetup`, `api/consent-forms`, archived once used; its name and version copied onto the record), the date signed, who signed (`ConsentSignedBy`: `Patient = 0`, `PersonWithParentalResponsibility = 1`, `BestInterestsDecision = 2`), who recorded it, an optional scan (a file on the pathway or a stage; "No scan" otherwise), and `ConsentStatus` (`Valid = 0`, `Withdrawn = 1`, `EnteredInError = 2`). Never renumber them. **MedApp records the clinician's statement; it never assesses capacity or validity** (the device decision). **Photo consent (MC27, `ClinicalPhotography`):** a Valid record on the form named "Clinical photography" (any version) anywhere on the pathway; a `NeedsConsent` stage whose name has "photograph" waits for it instead of its own record.
  - **Endpoints:** `GET`/`POST api/pathways/{id}/consent-records` (read `ViewPatients`, recording `PathwayViewed`; record `RecordConsent`: an open pathway, a stage neither finished nor skipped, the record able to change), `POST api/consent-records/{id}/withdraw` `{withdrawnOn, detail, expectedVersion}` (`RecordConsent`; allowed while restricted and in any pathway status), `.../entered-in-error` `{detail, expectedVersion}` (whoever recorded it within 24 hours, or `CorrectRecords`; soft-deletes the scan with an `AttachmentDeleted` entry), `.../scan` `{attachmentId, expectedVersion}` (one without a scan). Every change carries `expectedVersion` (409 when stale; the row version catches races). **Team walls** (MC5): reading needs the pathway seen (the recorded 404; `PathwayViewed` with `access`); every change is the pathway's own team's (409).
  - **Audit:** `ConsentRecorded = 42`, `ConsentWithdrawn = 43`, `ConsentEnteredInError = 44` (`ConsentChange`, not `PathwayChange`: `LastUpdated` doesn't move): the record's ID, form name and version, `signedBy` by name, `signedOn`, the scan's ID and statuses; **never the detail**. Timeline kind `Consent` (the detail for admins only).
  - **Elsewhere:** a scan can't be deleted on its own (409); a pathway with consent records can't be deleted (409); a withdrawal after its stage finished is flagged (`withdrawnAfterStageFinished`), nothing more. **Skipping a `NeedsConsent` stage isn't gated, on purpose:** a skipped stage wasn't done, so it needs no consent. Permission `RecordConsent` (Clinician, Senior clinician, Nurse; not Admin). Web: the pathway page's Consent card, Consent Forms (`/admin/consent-forms`), Needs consent on the stage library.
- **A pathway with specimens can't be deleted** (409).
- **A pathway has several notes** (`PatientPathwayNote`, Markdown, 4,000 characters), newest first, each with author, written and last edited times. `PATCH` refuses notes (400, `UpdatePatientPathwayValidator.NotesMovedMessage`, so an old page is told, not silently ignored), and the Edit Pathway page has none. A new pathway's notes become its first note, by its creator (`PathwayCreation.Record`).
  - **Endpoints, for staff:** `POST .../patient-pathways/{id}/notes` `{text}` (201, `Location` the pathway) and `PUT .../notes/{noteId}` `{text, expectedVersion}`. Notes come only in the pathway's response (`NoteList`, each with `CanEdit`, plus `CanAddNotes`), so reading them is its `PathwayViewed`. **`Notes` stays a string** (deprecated: the first note's or the old column's text); never change an existing response field's type.
  - **When:** whenever details can be edited (`PathwayWorkflow.CanChangeNotes`), so Blocked and **Completed** too; not Cancelled or Archived (409). Restricted or anonymised records: the usual `GetForUpdateOrThrowAsync` 409.
  - **Who:** staff add; **only the author edits** (403 otherwise, admins included: what someone wrote stays theirs). **Only admins delete;** staff ask. A note migrated with no known creator has no author ("Unknown") and can't be edited.
  - **Versions:** every add and edit writes a `PatientPathwayNoteVersion` (text, who, when; no foreign keys, patient and pathway copied in). An edit from a stale `expectedVersion` is a 409 (`PathwayNotes.ChangedMeanwhileMessage`); the row version catches simultaneous saves.
  - **Audit:** `NoteAdded = 13` and `NoteEdited = 14`, through `PathwayChange.AddNote`/`EditNote` (moving `LastUpdated`; the monitor is told, though no rule watches them). **Details hold the note's ID and version, never its text** (DPIA risk 4).
  - **Deleting is soft, by admins, with a reason** (`POST .../notes/{noteId}/delete` `{reason, detail?, expectedVersion}`, 204; `PathwayChange.DeleteNote`), in any pathway status (it's a correction), never while restricted or anonymised.
    - **Reason:** `NoteDeletionReason`, stored by number, so never renumber it: `WrongPatient = 0`, `WrittenInError = 1`, `Duplicate = 2`, `Other = 3` (words in `NoteDeletionReasons`). Detail: up to 500 characters, required for Other.
    - **Effect:** sets `DeletedAtUtc`, `DeletedByUserId`, `DeletionReason`, `DeletionDetail`; hidden from the pathway page (`PathwayNoteMapper.ToResponses`, and the `Notes` string), but the row and versions stay for history, the timeline and the export. Editing or deleting it again is a 409 (`PathwayNotes.DeletedMessage`).
    - **The version read:** every delete carries `expectedVersion`; a note edited since is a 409 (`PathwayNotes.EditedSinceOpenedMessage`). Keeping a note needs none.
    - **The author is told** (`AuthorsNoteDeleted`, see [Notifications](notifications.md)), so they can add it to the right record.
    - **The old field:** deleting a pathway's oldest note (the one #110's migration copied `PatientPathways.Notes` into) also clears that column (audit `oldNotesFieldCleared`).
    - **Audit:** `NoteDeleted = 15`: note ID, latest version, `deletionReason` by name, detail, `requestId` if any; **never the text**. Anonymising replaces the detail (`detail` is a `PatientAnonymiser` free-text key).
    - **Per-note flags:** `CanDelete` (admins, while the record can change), `CanRequestDeletion` (others, while it can change and nothing is waiting) and `DeletionRequestId` (the waiting request). `PathwayActor.Permissions` (from the session) carries the viewer's permissions; `CanDelete` needs `CorrectRecords`, and `CanEdit` and `CanRequestDeletion` need `WorkPathways`. The pathway's own `Can...` flags follow the viewer's permissions too ([Auth](auth.md), Roles and permissions).
  - **Staff request a deletion** (`POST .../notes/{noteId}/deletion-requests` `{reason, detail?}`, 201 with the note; `RequestNoteDeletionHandler`): any note, any status, not while restricted or anonymised.
    - **The request:** `PathwayNoteDeletionRequest` (`MedApp.Application/Pathways/NoteDeletionRequests`; no foreign keys): note, pathway, patient, requester (ID and username then), reason, detail, status, timestamps and decider. `NoteDeletionRequestStatus` is stored by number, so never renumber it: `Pending = 0`, `Approved = 1`, `Declined = 2`, `Closed = 3` (no decision).
    - **One waiting per note:** `PendingDeletionRequestId` on the note; asking again is a 409 (`PathwayNotes.AlreadyRequestedMessage`). The note's row version and the filtered unique index `IX_PathwayNoteDeletionRequests_NoteId_Pending` stop races (`NoteDeletionRequestRepository` makes either the same 409).
    - **Saved together:** request and note share the DbContext (`INoteDeletionRequestRepository`), one `SaveChangesAsync`.
    - Every admin but the requester is told (`NoteDeletionNotifications`).
  - **An admin decides** (`GET api/pathways/note-deletion-requests/{id}`, `POST .../{id}/decision` `{deleteNote, expectedVersion?, reason?, detail?}`, admins). The GET gives the note's text, the patient (as `PatientBanner`), the pathway's title, request and outcome, `CanDecide` and `WhyCantDecide`, and records `PathwayViewed` (`PatientViewed` once the pathway is deleted). **Delete note** needs `expectedVersion` and uses the admin's reason and detail or else the requester's; **Keep note** declines. The requester is told. Already decided (`PathwayNotes.AlreadyDecidedMessage`), or a restricted or anonymised record, is a 409; the row version catches two admins.
    - **A direct delete approves** a waiting request (telling its requester).
    - **Deleting a pathway closes** its waiting requests (`Closed`, same save, `DeletePatientPathwayHandler`), untold.
    - **Anonymising** replaces request and deletion details and closes waiting requests; an older API copy doesn't (the chart's README, `docs/todo.md`).
    - **Privacy:** the export lists deleted notes and every request (`ExportedNoteDeletionRequestResponse`, loaded in `PatientRecordRepository`); `WrongPatient` deletions are flagged `IsAboutAnotherPatient`. Hazard H-38.
  - **The old `Notes` column** stays (`PatientPathway.LegacyNotes`, for the deprecated string) so older API copies work during a rollout (the chart's two-release rule). Migration `AddedProcedureNotes` copied it into a first note, by the creator, or "Unknown" if anyone else had edited it (`DetailsUpdated` with `changes.notes`). Anonymising clears it. Dropping it is a to-do, after copying text only it holds (`docs/todo.md`).
- Status numbers are stored, so never renumber `PatientPathwayStatus`, `PatientPathwayAuditAction`, `StageSkipReason` or `NoteDeletionRequestStatus`.
- **Three statuses are shown by another name than the code's** (`docs/glossary.md`): `InReview` is "Awaiting review", `Blocked` is "On hold" and `Completed` is "Discharged"; a `DueDate` is a "Target date". The enum members, DTO fields and audit details keep the code's names (Insights and the timeline read them: `"to":"Blocked"`). Put a status into words with `PathwayWorkflow.Describe` in the API and `statusWords` or `statusLabel` in the web app (`src/utils/statusWords.ts`), never by splitting its name.
