common-passwords.txt
====================

The 10,000 most common passwords: the first 10,000 lines (one blank line among them, which the app skips) of the
UK National Cyber Security Centre's list of the 100,000 most used passwords found in data breaches, as published in
SecLists.

- Original: National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC), "PwnedPasswordsTop100k.txt", from Troy Hunt's Have I Been Pwned
  Pwned Passwords data, published with the NCSC's blog post "Passwords, passwords everywhere" (April 2019):
  https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/static-assets/documents/PwnedPasswordsTop100k.txt
  Crown copyright, available under the Open Government Licence v3.0:
  https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/
- Copy used: SecLists (Daniel Miessler, Jason Haddix and contributors), MIT licence,
  Passwords/Common-Credentials/100k-most-used-passwords-NCSC.txt at commit
  180c2d55afa7aab8b18b7552f82dd0c92cc4ede5 (15 November 2026):
  https://github.com/danielmiessler/SecLists/blob/180c2d55afa7aab8b18b7552f82dd0c92cc4ede5/Passwords/Common-Credentials/100k-most-used-passwords-NCSC.txt
  (the NCSC's own address was unavailable when the list was taken, on 16 November 2026).
  SHA-256 of that whole file: c2e5696882c603b76bb67a47ee970897e5a76fc4c3f5547abe3d0ca340c576e0

To refresh it, take the first 10,000 lines of a newer copy, and update this notice.
