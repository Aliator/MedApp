# Referrals

Referrals, their history and their letters: read before changing any of them.

Concept and decisions: `docs/centre-phase2-plan.md` (decisions 8 to 17 and 43 to 50, section 9's PR 5, and section 10's later decisions). A referral is **an administrative record** of one request (usually from a GP practice) for the centre to see one patient in one specialty, typed by staff from the letter. **The clinician decides; the app records:** nothing is scored, suggested or ordered by clinical content (`docs/clinical-safety/intended-purpose-and-device-decision.md`).
- **A referral** (`Referral`, `MedApp.Domain/Referrals`; table `Referrals`, migration `AddedReferrals`): the patient and the specialty asked for (both foreign keys, restrict), `ReceivedOn` (a UK date, not in the future, at most 365 days back), the referring practice (free text, 200, required), the referring clinician (free text, 200, optional: a third party's personal data), the practice's ODS code (optional), the priority asked for (`PatientPathwayPriority`), the reason (free text, 2,000, clinical), the status, who registered it and when (user ID and username), the decision columns, `PathwayId`, `Version` and a row version.
  - **The decision columns** (`DecidedAtUtc`/`ByUserId`/`ByUsername`, `RejectionReason`, `DecisionDetail` (500), `RedirectedTo` (200)) are filled today only by withdrawing and entering in error (`DecisionDetail`); triage fills the rest.
  - **ODS codes** (`OdsCodes`): 3 to 10 letters and digits once spaces are removed, stored upper case, **format only** (MedApp has no link to the ODS).
- **Where the rules live** (`MedApp.Application/Referrals/Common`):
  - `ReferralWorkflow`: open and closed statuses (only `AwaitingTriage` is open), what can be done in each, and every message (`ChangedMeanwhileMessage`, `RestrictedRegisterMessage`, `PossibleDuplicateMessage`, `TriageNotAvailableMessage`, ...). `WhyCantEdit`, `WhyCantClose`, `WhyFilesCantChange`, `ReadOnlyReason`.
  - `ReferralChange`: **the only way to change a referral.** It applies the change, bumps `Version` and writes its `ReferralEvent`s, which the repository saves in the same `SaveChangesAsync`.
  - `ReferralRules` (the validators' shared checks), `ReferralMapper` (with `ReferralResponses`; flags `CanEdit`, `CanClose`, `CanAttachFiles`, `CanTriage` (always false yet), `WhyCantTriage`, `ReadOnlyReason`), `ReferralRepositoryExtensions` (`GetForEditOrThrowAsync`, `GetForCloseOrThrowAsync`, `GetOrThrowAsync`).
  - `ReferralSearch` (`Queries/SearchReferrals`): LINQ that runs in SQL and in memory, ending in `ThenBy(r => r.Id)`.
  - `IReferralRepository` (`Referrals/Repositories`): shares the request's DbContext with the pathway repository, so triage can save the referral and its new pathway together.
- **Endpoints** (`ReferralsController`, `api/referrals`):
  - `POST api/referrals` (`RegisterReferrals`; 201) `{patientId, specialtyId, receivedOn, referrerPractice, referrerClinician?, referrerOdsCode?, requestedPriority, reason, confirmPossibleDuplicate}`.
  - `GET api/referrals/search` (`ViewPatients`; a `PagedResponse`): `statuses`, `specialtyIds`, `mine` (the viewer's specialties; every specialty if they have none), `patientId`, `search` (the patient's name), `sort` (`receivedOn`, the default, `patient`, `specialty`, `priority`, `registered`) and `ascending`.
  - `GET api/referrals/awaiting-triage/count` (`ViewPatients`): the side nav's count, in the viewer's specialties (everyone's if they have none). **Records nothing:** a count names nobody.
  - `GET api/referrals/{id}` (`ViewPatients`): the referral with its history, letter count, flags and the patient's banner details and flags.
  - `PUT api/referrals/{id}` (`RegisterReferrals`): edit while awaiting triage, with `expectedVersion`.
  - `POST api/referrals/{id}/withdraw` and `…/entered-in-error` (`RegisterReferrals`) `{detail, expectedVersion}`: awaiting triage only; the detail is required (500).
- **Versions:** every change names `expectedVersion`; another change since is a 409 (`ReferralWorkflow.ChangedMeanwhileMessage`), and the row version catches two saves at once.
- **Duplicates are shown, never merged** (hazard H-44): registering looks for the patient's open referrals to the same specialty (awaiting triage, or accepted with the pathway still open); without `ConfirmPossibleDuplicate` it's a 400 on that field, naming when each was received and its status, **never its text** (`PossibleDuplicateMessage`).
- **Editing** while awaiting triage may change the specialty (decision 13's correction): the event records `DetailsEdited` with the specialty's old and new name. `SpecialtyChanged` is kept for triage's redirect.
- **History** (`ReferralEvent`, table `ReferralEvents`): no foreign keys; **structured values only** (statuses, the specialty's ID and name, the date received, the priority, the names of the fields edited), **never free text**: the reason, the referrer and the details live only on the referral, so an edit keeps no old text. Referral changes are recorded here, **not also in the activity log**.
- **Stored enums, never renumbered:** `ReferralStatus` (`AwaitingTriage = 0`, `Accepted = 1`, `Rejected = 2`, `RedirectedElsewhere = 3`, `Withdrawn = 4`, `EnteredInError = 5`), `ReferralRejectionReason` (`NotOfferedHere = 0`, `DoesNotMeetCriteria = 1`, `InsufficientInformation = 2`, `Duplicate = 3`, `Other = 4`) and `ReferralEventAction` (`Registered = 0` to `TargetChanged = 11`, all declared now; only `Registered`, `DetailsEdited`, `Withdrawn` and `EnteredInError` are written yet). `ActivityAction.ReferralViewed = 43`.
- **Restricted and anonymised records** ([privacy](privacy.md)): registering, editing and attaching are refused (409). The restricted message tells staff to give the letter to an admin now, so it isn't lost (hazard H-40). **Withdrawing and marking entered in error stay allowed while restricted** (so care can be stopped safely), and are refused once anonymised (`WhyCantClose`). Restricting a patient counts their referrals awaiting triage in the confirmation (`ConfirmOpenPathways`).
- **Privacy:**
  - **Reads:** opening a referral records `ReferralViewed` against the patient with `{referralId}` in the details, through `IActivityLog.RecordViewAsync` (deduplicated per person and referral over the view window; fails closed). The search records `PatientListed` for each patient shown. Every GET is classified in `EndpointCoverageTests`.
  - **Free text** (the reason, the referrer, the details) never reaches a staff timeline, a notification, an email or a log line. Staff with `ViewPatients` read it on the referral page.
  - **Export and anonymisation:** `PatientRecordRepository` loads referrals and their history. The export gives `referrals` (every field, staff by username) and `referralHistory` (without staff IDs); letters show as attached to the referral. Anonymising is **refused while any referral awaits triage**; it replaces the reason, practice, clinician, decision detail and redirect destination with placeholders and clears the ODS code (dates, statuses, priority and specialty stay); history details are scrubbed as others are.
  - **Deleting:** a patient with any referral can't be deleted (409). Deleting a pathway clears its referral's `PathwayId` in the same save.
  - **Retention** (`RetentionSchedule`): the referral history counts towards the conclusion of treatment (`LastReferralChangeUtc`), and a patient with a referral awaiting triage isn't a retention candidate.
- **The letter** is an attachment with a fourth target, `ReferralId` ([attachments](attachments.md)): `?referralId=` on list and upload, every attachment rule, listed with the patient's files. Not on a referral entered in error (`WhyFilesCantChange`); in any other status, since a letter can arrive after triage.
- **Timeline** ([timeline](timeline.md)): a third feed, kind `Referrals`, actions `Referral<Action>` (`ReferralRegistered`, ...). The overview lists referrals awaiting triage (`awaitingReferrals`).
- **Specialties** ([specialties](specialties.md)): a specialty with referrals is in use (archived, never deleted); archiving one with referrals awaiting triage is a 409.
- **Not notified:** no notification or email for referrals yet. Triage adds `ReferralTriaged = 23`.
- **The web app:** the Referrals page (`/referrals`: Awaiting triage, oldest received first; Closed; All), its side nav item with the awaiting count, Register Referral (`/referrals/new`: find or add the patient, duplicates shown inline), the referral page (banner, details, letter, history; edit, withdraw and entered in error, each confirmation with the patient banner), the patient page's Referrals card, the timeline's entries and the export's section. Its actions follow the response's flags; Register Referral shows to everyone signed in until `useCan` lands (`docs/todo.md`), and the server refuses with a 403.
- **Not built yet:** triage (accept, reject, redirect, `TriageReferrals` with specialty membership, `ReferralTriaged`: PR 6), the waiting-time clock (PR 7), filters on the pathway list and Insights (PR 8), and demo data (PR 9).
