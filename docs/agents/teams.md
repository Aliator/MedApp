# Teams

The centre's teams (its clinics and the lab), each template's and pathway's team, staff memberships, account types and **the team walls**: read before changing any of them, and **before any query on team data**.

**Called specialties until MC2** (`docs/multi-clinic-plan.md`; migration `RenamedSpecialtiesToTeams`, script `tools/rename-specialties-to-teams.py`, which brings a branch from before across). Screens say "team" looking inward and "clinic" or "the lab" sending outward (a referral's Clinic field). Stored names keep the old word: a referral history's `specialtyId`/`specialtyName`/`previousSpecialty…` and the edited field `specialty`, an MDT audit entry's `specialty`, and saved searches' `specialty` (read as `team` by `searchNow`, `beforePathways.ts`). `ActivityAction.TeamMembershipChanged` keeps 42.

Concept and decisions: `docs/centre-phase2-plan.md` (decisions 1 to 7) and `docs/multi-clinic-plan.md` (decisions 181 to 210, MC3 and MC4). A team decides what its members see (the team walls, below); it decides nothing clinical.
- **A team** (`Team`, `MedApp.Domain/Teams`) is a name (unique ignoring case, 100 characters), archived or not, with a `Version` and a **kind** (`TeamKind`: `Clinic = 0`, `Lab = 1`; `TeamResponse.kind`). At most one lab that isn't archived (filtered unique index `IX_Teams_OneLab`): **the lab** (`Team.LabId`, seeded "Lab", renameable); teams made on the Teams page are clinics. `ITeamRepository.GetLabAsync` finds it. The rules and messages are in `MedApp.Application/Teams/Common` (`TeamRules`, `TeamMapper`, `TeamUsage`); the repository is `ITeamRepository`.
  - **Endpoints:** `GET api/teams` (`includeArchived`) and `GET api/teams/{id}`, `UseApp` (anyone with a role); `POST`, `PUT api/teams/{id}` `{name, expectedVersion}`, `POST …/{id}/archive` and `…/restore` `{expectedVersion}`, and `DELETE …/{id}`, `ManageSetup`. Each response says how many templates, pathways, referrals and members of staff have it, and `CanBeDeleted`.
  - **Versions:** every change names the version the caller saw; another admin's change since is a 409 (`TeamRules.ChangedMeanwhileMessage`). `Version` is also a concurrency token: the repository's update sets its original value to the version read, so two saves at once can't both win.
  - **In use, archived, never deleted:** a team any template, pathway, referral or member of staff has can't be deleted (409 naming what has it; the foreign keys refuse too, if something took it meanwhile). An archived one can't be chosen for a template or newly given to anyone (409, `TeamRules.CheckChosenAsync`); whatever already has it keeps it. **Archiving one with referrals awaiting triage is a 409** (`TeamRules.AwaitingTriageMessage`, saying how many), so none is stranded (hazard H-40). `TeamUsage` counts its referrals (`ReferralCount`) and those awaiting triage (`AwaitingTriageCount`).
  - **General** (`Team.GeneralId`, seeded through `HasData`, like the default stage definition): migration `AddedSpecialties` put every existing template and pathway in it. It's the database's default for both `TeamId` columns, so an API copy from before teams still creates them (in General) during a rollout. Admins may rename, archive or (once nothing has it) delete it.
- **Every pathway template belongs to one** (`PathwayTemplate.TeamId`, required on create: 400 without; `PATCH` takes an optional `teamId` to move it). The response gives `teamId` and `teamName`.
- **A pathway copies its template's** when it's created (`PathwayCreation.FromTemplate` sets `PatientPathway.TeamId`), as it copies the stages; moving the template later moves only new pathways. The pathway's response gives `teamId`. Lists and Insights filter on the pathway's own copy: the pathway search's `TeamIds` (any of; the list's and My Worklist's `team` filter) and Insights' `TeamId` (`InsightsScope`); see [Pathways](pathways.md) and [Insights](insights.md).
- **Staff belong to any number** (`TeamMembership`, keyed by user and team, with a **role**: `TeamMemberRole` `Member = 0`, `ClinicAdmin = 1`; deleted with the account by the foreign key's cascade). `GET`/`PUT api/auth/users/{username}/teams` `{teams: [{teamId, role}]}` (`ManageUsers`; the PUT sets exactly that set, a team given twice counts once, and returns them) and `GET api/auth/me/teams` (`UseApp`, their own); each answers `{id, name, isArchived, kind, role}`. Handlers: `SetUserTeams`, `GetUserTeams`, `GetMyTeams` under `MedApp.Application/Authentication/Users`.
  - **Refused:** a role other than `Member`/`ClinicAdmin` (400, "Choose Member or Clinic admin."); an admin in a team, or someone in a team given Admin (409, `TeamRules.AdminHasNoTeam`/`TeamMemberIsNoAdmin`, decision 209: someone who is both has two accounts); a clinic admin of the lab (409, `TeamRules.LabHasNoClinicAdmin`).
  - **Logged:** a change (of teams or roles) records `TeamMembershipChanged = 42` in the activity log (the account as target; the teams' names before and after, a clinic admin's marked " (clinic admin)", their IDs and roles), saved in the same `SaveChangesAsync` as the memberships (`ITeamRepository.SetForUserAsync`). Setting what someone already has records nothing.
  - **Triage** needs the `TriageReferrals` permission **and** membership of the referral's team ([referrals](referrals.md)). Membership alone grants nothing.
  - **Who can triage each:** `TeamResponse.TriagerCount` counts its members who hold `TriageReferrals` (`TeamUsage.TriagerCount`, through `RolePermissionQueries.HoldersOf`); the Teams page warns about any that isn't archived and has none (hazard H-40).
- **Waiting-time targets:** each team has one per priority, in days (`TargetDaysUrgent`, `TargetDaysHigh`, `TargetDaysMedium`, `TargetDaysLow`; 1 to 365; a new one 14, 28, 126 and 126 unless given; on `POST` and `PUT`, each left out keeps its value). They're the centre's own administrative targets: a referral's clock copies its priority's ([referrals](referrals.md)), and changing them moves no clock already running.
- **Account types** (`AccountTypes`, decision 182; worked out, never stored): `Admin` (holds Admin), `ClinicAdmin` (a clinic admin of any team), else `Staff`. On `whoami` (`accountType`, and `teams` as `GET me/teams` gives them) and each `UserResponse`. A clinic admin's extras are a Stage 2 to-do (S2-9).
- **Admins are read only** (decision 208): `Permissions.NotForAdmin` holds every clinical permission (the web app's `NOT_FOR_ADMIN`); Admin keeps setup, accounts, oversight, meetings, calendars, working hours, the governance actions and `ViewAllTeams` (Administrator-only: every team's records, read only).

## The team walls (MC4)

Every query on team data takes the viewer's scope (MC4: pathways, the timeline, files; MC5: referrals, appointments, calendars, letters, results, consent, MDT items, Insights and the counts; samples with the lab's MC14).
- **The scope** (`TeamScope`, `MedApp.Application/Common/Teams`): `TeamIds`, `SeesAllTeams` (`ViewAllTeams`), `IsSystem` (jobs, the seed: never a request). Built per request from the session: `SessionService` reads the memberships with the roles, one `medapp:team` claim each (`SessionAuthenticationDefaults.TeamClaim`), `User.ToTeamScope()`. Carried on `PathwayActor.Scope` and `CalendarActor.Scope` (their `Teams`; null only outside a request, which is the system's) and on each query of the walled areas (`Scope`/`Teams`).
- **The rules** (`TeamAccess`, one place, LINQ that runs in SQL and in memory): `Pathways(scope, grants)` (one `EXISTS` on `PathwayAccess`), `ByPathway<T>` (a record's team is the viewer's, or its pathway is seen), `ByTeam<T>`, `CanChange` (only the owner team, or the system: admins and the lab read), `Strongest` (owner, stage owner, consult, MDT, admin). Not global query filters: clash checks, jobs and uniqueness checks read every team's rows.
- **Grants** (`PathwayAccess`, `PathwayAccessReason` `Owner = 0`, `StageOwner = 1`, `Consult = 2`, `Mdt = 3`; no foreign key to the pathway): written only in the same save as their cause: `Owner` with the pathway (`PathwayChange.GrantOwner`, from `PathwayCreation.Record`), `StageOwner` for the lab with a pathway's first sample (`SpecimenChange.PassToLab`) and when a stage it owns is reached (MC7, `PathwayChange.ReachNext`, once per team), never ended (decision 184); `Consult` for the asked team with a consult, ended when it's closed or withdrawn (MC25, `ConsultChange`, [pathways](pathways.md)); `Mdt` for each MDT attendee by person, per listed item (`MdtItemId`; MC26, `MdtGrants`), ended once the item is discussed or withdrawn and the meeting has ended (the `mdt-grants` job), or the MDT is cancelled. Read through `IPathwayAccessRepository`.
- **At one record** (`ITeamBoundary`, the shared caller-scope service): `EnsurePathwayVisibleAsync`, `EnsureCanChangePathwayAsync`, `EnsureTeamVisibleAsync`, `EnsureCanChangeTeamAsync`, `VisiblePathwaysAsync`, `RefuseAsync`. **Across a wall is a 404** with the words of a missing record, recording `TeamBoundaryRefused = 54` (`{targetKind, targetId}`, once per target in the view window); seen but another team's is a 409 (`TeamWalls.NotYourTeams`).
- **Pathway commands** load through `PathwayRepositoryExtensions.GetForUpdateOrThrowAsync`/`GetForPauseOrThrowAsync` with the boundary and the actor's scope (owner team only), or `GetForCorrectionOrThrowAsync` (an admin's correction: seen is enough); stage work loads through `GetForStageWorkOrThrowAsync` (the stage's team: `TeamAccess.CanWorkStage`, MC7) and notes through `GetForNotesOrThrowAsync` (also the team working a current stage); the scope-less overloads are for system work and MC5's areas. A pathway starts only from one of the creator's teams' templates (400 on `templateId`). Responses' flags and `readOnlyReason` follow the walls (`PathwayMapper`'s `viewerScope`).
- **Reads:** pathway search, labels and a patient's pathways filter in SQL (`IPatientPathwayRepository` methods take the scope); the patient record and the timeline's overview list another team's pathways only as `otherTeamPathways` (`OtherTeamPathways`: team, `InProgress`/`Completed`, since; not cancelled or archived; none for admins); the timeline leaves out other teams' entries (`TeamId` on every audit, appointment and referral history entry); files follow `ByPathway` (`AttachmentWalls`). `PathwayViewed` records `access`.
- **Every clinical row has its team** (`TeamId`, foreign key `NoAction`, indexed): appointments, files, letters, results, consent, samples, messages to patients, and the history entries (copied from their subject). Set at creation: the pathway's, a referral's, an appointment's, or the uploader's or booker's team (`TeamScope.DefaultTeamId`) for one without.
- **Tests:** `TeamAccessTests`, `TeamAccessSqlTests`, `TeamBoundaryTests`, `TeamScopeConventionTests`, `PathwayTeamWallsTests`, `TimelineTeamWallsTests`, `PatientTeamWallsTests`. The integration leak suites (`MedApp.IntegrationTests/Teams`): `TeamBoundaryCoverageTests` classifies every endpoint (`Areas`; Administrator-only found from its permissions) and probes each team-scoped one as team B with team A's records (`TeamLeakTest.WithTeamAIds`: a read is a 404, nothing names A's records, no change to A's rows); `TeamLeakCanaryTests` reads every endpoint as team B, the lab and a consulted team after the consult closed, looking for A's marker text. A new team-scoped endpoint needs its route's parameters in `WithTeamAIds`.
- **MC5's areas** (each area file has its detail): a record follows its pathway, or its own `TeamId` when it has none (`ITeamBoundary.EnsureRecordVisibleAsync` at one record, returning the `access`; `TeamAccess.ByPathway` in lists: `ReferralSearch.Visible`, `LetterSearch.Visible`, `ResultSearch`, `MdtWalls`, `AppointmentSearch`). Another team's by ID is the recorded 404; a change is the owning team's (409 `TeamWalls.RecordNotYours`, "This referral/letter/result/appointment…"), except corrections (a result entered in error, a letter withdrawn by `CorrectRecords`) and an MDT outcome, which whoever sees the record may make by their permissions. Views record `access`; an admin's list views `{access: "Admin", teamFilter}` (`TeamPicker.ListDetails`). The side nav and home counts count only what the viewer may see. `PauseClock`/`ResumeClock` load the pathway through the walled guards.
- **The clinic picker (MC6, `TeamPicker`):** `TeamId` on the patients, referrals, appointments, letters and results searches and Insights; staff only their own teams (400 on `TeamId`, "Choose one of your own teams, or all of them."), admins any. Patients: "has a pathway, referral or appointment in that team" (`PatientSearch.InTeam`). Insights: `InsightsScope.TeamIds` is the viewer's teams unless they see every team.
- **Calendars (MC11; [calendar](calendar.md)):** another team's appointments and meetings that aren't yours are `otherTeamsBusy` on `CalendarResponse` (`what` "Busy" or "Meeting", no patient or ID; `CalendarWalls`); clashes still read every team's rows. `GET api/calendar/team` (`UseApp`) takes `TeamId`. Booking: the clinician must be in the appointment's team (400 on `StaffUserId`); one without a pathway takes the booker's `DefaultTeamId`. Meetings: everyone invited shares a team with the organiser (400 on `AttendeeUserIds`), except an MDT or an admin's; another's meeting is a recorded 404 (`MeetingWalls`).
- **Tests:** `TeamPickerTests`, `WalledAreaQueriesConventionTests` and each area's `…TeamWallsTests`; fixtures `TeamWallsTestBoundary`, `GrantsTeamBoundary`.
- **The web app:** the Teams page (`/admin/teams`, under Admin after Users), the template editor's Team field, the Users page's Teams dialog, and the read-only list in Account Settings.
- **Demo data:** `DemoTeams` (`tools/MedApp.DevMcp/DemoData`) makes Dermatology, Gynaecology, Urology, Diabetes and Biomechanics through the commands, puts each template in its own, and gives the clinicians theirs (not the clinical lead or the service manager, who are admins: MC12 moves their clinical work); General and the lab stay.
