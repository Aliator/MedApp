# Notifications

In-app notifications, their kinds and live delivery: read before adding or changing a notification.

- Handlers call `INotificationSender.SendAsync(new Notification { … })` (`MedApp.Application/Notifications/Common`) after their own save. It stores the notification, then pushes it to every page the user has open.
- **Live delivery is SignalR:** `NotificationsHub` at `/api/notifications/hub`, by user ID (the session's `NameIdentifier`), through `SignalRNotificationPublisher`. Best effort: a failure is logged, and the notification shows on the next load.
- **API copies share a backplane:** `ConnectionStrings:SignalRBackplane`, a Redis-compatible server (`SignalRBackplane`): Valkey in Kubernetes, unset in development. Browsers use WebSocket only, without negotiating (`LiveNotifications`), as the two requests could reach different copies.
- `GET api/notifications` returns the newest and the unread count; `POST api/notifications/read` marks all, `POST api/notifications/{id}/read` one.
- **Kinds** (`NotificationKind`) are stored by number, so never renumber them: 0 assigned a pathway (not by yourself); `AppointmentBooked = 1`, `AppointmentRescheduled = 2`, `AppointmentCancelled = 3` (see [Calendar and appointments](calendar.md)); `MeetingInvited = 4`, `MeetingChanged = 5`, `MeetingCancelled = 6`, `MeetingDeclined = 7` (to the organiser), `DayClosed = 8` (a day closed with something of theirs on it), `AppointmentRecorded = 9` (one recorded after the event) (1 to 9 have fixed titles); 10 to 17 monitoring, one per `MonitoringEvent`, the service alerts `MonitoredReferralNotTriaged = 32`, `MonitoredResultNotSignedOff = 33`, `MonitoredVisitNotBooked = 34`, `MonitoredSpecimenOverdue = 35`, `MonitoredWaitBreached = 36`, `MonitoredPauseReviewPassed = 37` (one per rule and run, a count; `SubjectId` is the rule's specialty; [Monitoring](monitoring.md)) and `MonitoredStageFinishedWithoutVisit = 38` (`Notification.IsMonitoring` is a set: 10 to 17 and 32 to 38); 18 to 21 note deletion (`Notification.IsNoteDeletion`); `StageVisitWithdrawn = 22` (a finished stage's only visit withdrawn as entered in error, to the pathway's assignees and the holders of `FinishWithoutVisit`; a fixed title); `ReferralTriaged = 23` (to whoever registered a referral, when someone else triages it: "Referral triaged", the triager's username, the outcome and the specialty; it carries `ReferralId` and opens the referral: [referrals](referrals.md)); `SpecimenRejectedByLab = 24` and `SpecimenLost = 25` (a specimen the user asked for or took, never to the actor; fixed titles "Specimen rejected" and "Specimen lost", the type's name only: [Specimens](specimens.md)); `ScheduledJobFailing = 40` (a scheduled job failed three times in a row, to every holder of `ManageSetup`, once until it succeeds; a fixed title; links to Scheduled Jobs: `docs/agents/jobs.md`); `DataRequestDueSoon = 42` (an open data request due within 7 days, to every holder of `ManagePrivacy`, once per due date, by the daily `data-request-reminders` job; a fixed title, the due date only; its `SubjectId` is the request, opened at `/data-requests/{id}`: [privacy](privacy.md)). No foreign keys; they carry `AppointmentId`, `ReferralId`, a meeting's day (`CalendarDate`) and, but for a cancellation, its `MeetingId` (linking to that Calendar day with the meeting open, `?meeting=<id>`) or `NoteDeletionRequestId`, and, for newer kinds, one `SubjectId` whose meaning the kind sets (the specimen for 24 and 25; decision 72), which the web app links to its page.
  - **Note deletion** (`NoteDeletionNotifications`): `NoteDeletionRequested = 18` to every admin but the requester (with `PathwayId` and `NoteDeletionRequestId`, opening the request's dialog); `NoteDeletionApproved = 19` and `NoteDeletionDeclined = 20` to the requester unless they decided; `AuthorsNoteDeleted = 21` to the author unless they deleted it, asked for it (they get 19) or aren't known. Fixed titles (`Notification.HasFixedTitle`, so anonymising leaves them), usernames only: never the note, reason or patient.
- **Preferences** (`NotificationPreferences`, Account Settings, `GET`/`PUT api/notifications/preferences`): only whether to be told of being assigned (appointment kinds can't be switched off yet). `NotificationSender` checks them for optional kinds (`NotificationPreferences.IsOptional`); no row means everything. Kept on the server because it reads them.
- **Notification text never carries free text or the patient.** Titles are fixed phrases; messages use usernames, enums, admin-written names (templates, stages, meeting titles) and dates. (The older assignment notification still uses the pathway's title: DPIA risk 3.)
  - **Meeting titles count as admin-written:** only admins arrange meetings, and the Title field says no patient details. Notifications quote them and outlive the meeting, beyond export and anonymisation (DPIA risk 25). Meeting notes are never in a notification.
